# House of Ascania
> A dynasty of German rulers whose members included the princes of Anhalt-Dessau and Anhalt-Zerbst

## Key Facts
- John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst was a member of this house
- Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau was a member of this house
- Karl I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst was a member of this house
- Was a dynasty of German rulers
- Its members included princes of Anhalt-Dessau
- Its members included princes of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Is a broad category and does not itself state individual birth or death dates
