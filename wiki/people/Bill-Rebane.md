# Bill Rebane
> American film director known for low-budget films who co-directed Monster a Go-Go

## Key Facts
- Co-directed the 1965 film Monster a Go-Go
- American film director born in 1937 in Riga, Latvia
- Best known for low-budget films
- Co-directed the 1965 film Monster a Go-Go with Herschell Gordon Lewis
- Directed the 1979 American horror film The Capture of Bigfoot
