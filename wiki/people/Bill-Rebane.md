# Bill Rebane
> American film director known for low-budget films who directed The Capture of Bigfoot

## Key Facts
- Co-directed the 1965 film Monster a Go-Go with Herschell Gordon Lewis
- Born in 1937 in Riga, Latvia
- American film director
- Best known for low-budget films
- Directed the 1979 horror film The Capture of Bigfoot
