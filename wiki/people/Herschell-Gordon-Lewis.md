# Herschell Gordon Lewis
> American filmmaker known as the Godfather of Gore who co-directed Monster a Go-Go

## Key Facts
- Co-directed the 1965 film Monster a Go-Go with Bill Rebane
- American filmmaker often called the Godfather of Gore
- Born on 15 June 1926 in Chicago, Illinois
- Died on 26 September 2016
