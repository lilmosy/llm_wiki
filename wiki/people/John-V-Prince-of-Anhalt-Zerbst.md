# John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled Anhalt-Zerbst

## Key Facts
- Was a German prince of the House of Ascania
- Born on 4 September 1504 in Dessau
- Died on 4 February 1551 in Zerbst
- Son of Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau
- His eldest son was Karl I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the son of Margarete of Munsterberg
- Married Margaret of Brandenburg, daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
