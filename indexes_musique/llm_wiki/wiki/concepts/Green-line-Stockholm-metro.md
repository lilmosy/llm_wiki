# Green line (Stockholm metro)
> One of the lines of the Stockholm metro, whose east and west parts were connected in 1957 via Gamla stan station.

## Key Facts
- Serves Gamla stan metro station.
- East and west parts of the Green line were connected on 24 November 1957 via the new stretch between Slussen and Hötorget, which included Gamla stan station.
