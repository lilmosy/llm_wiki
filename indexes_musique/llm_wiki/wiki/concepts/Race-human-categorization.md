# Race (human categorization)
> A conceptual framework for categorizing humans that is examined through genetic studies of population differentiation.

## Key Facts
- Discussed in relation to genetic studies examining selection and differentiation among human populations.
- Coop et al. (2009) is cited as evidence relevant to understanding patterns of genetic variation across populations, informing debates about race as a biological category.
