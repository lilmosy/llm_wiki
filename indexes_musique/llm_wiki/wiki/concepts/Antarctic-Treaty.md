# Antarctic Treaty
> An international treaty affecting the status of territorial claims in Antarctica, including Australia's claim over Wilkes Land.

## Key Facts
- Australia is a signatory to this treaty
- During the period of its operation, the validity of Australia's claim to Wilkes Land as part of the Australian Antarctic Territory has been placed in abeyance
