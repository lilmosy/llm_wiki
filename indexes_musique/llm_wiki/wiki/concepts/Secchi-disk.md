# Secchi disk
> A simple circular disk device used to measure water transparency or turbidity by determining the depth at which it becomes invisible.

## Key Facts
- Created in 1865 by Angelo Secchi
- Originally a plain white, circular disk of a set diameter
- Mounted on a pole or line and lowered slowly into water
- The depth at which the disk is no longer visible is called the Secchi depth
- Secchi depth is used as a measure of water transparency, related to turbidity
- A modified, smaller diameter, black-and-white version has also been used to measure freshwater transparency
