## Bird Species
- [[Common-nightingale]] (Nightingale, Luscinia megarhynchos) -- A migratory insectivorous bird species that breeds in Europe and south-west Asia and winters in sub-Saharan Africa. #bird-species #migratory-bird #insectivore
- [[Thrush-nightingale]] (Luscinia luscinia) -- A bird species closely related to the common nightingale, with a more northerly distribution. #bird-species #migratory-bird

## International Law
- [[1982-United-Nations-Convention-on-the-Law-of-the-Sea]] (UNCLOS 1982, UNCLOS) -- An international treaty that establishes the legal definition of territorial waters and related maritime rights. #international-law #treaty #maritime-law
- [[Maritime-delimitation]] (Maritime delimitation) -- The international legal term for the adjustment of maritime boundaries, such as those of territorial waters. #international-law #maritime-law #boundaries

## Maritime Law
- [[Territorial-waters]] (Territorial sea, territorial sea) -- A belt of coastal waters extending up to 12 nautical miles from a coastal state's baseline, considered sovereign territory. #maritime-law #international-law #sovereignty

## Oceanography
