# Common nightingale
> A migratory insectivorous bird species that breeds in Europe and south-west Asia and winters in sub-Saharan Africa.

## Key Facts
- Breeds in forest and scrub habitats in Europe and south-west Asia
- Winters in sub-Saharan Africa
- Is not found naturally in the Americas
- Has a more southerly distribution than the closely related thrush nightingale
- Nests on or near the ground in dense vegetation
- Research conducted in Germany identified geographical factors defining favoured breeding habitat for this species
