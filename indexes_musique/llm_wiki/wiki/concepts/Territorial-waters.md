# Territorial waters
> A belt of coastal waters extending up to 12 nautical miles from a coastal state's baseline, considered sovereign territory.

## Key Facts
- Defined by the 1982 United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea
- Extends at most 12 nautical miles (22.2 km; 13.8 mi) from the baseline, usually the mean low-water mark, of a coastal state
- Regarded as the sovereign territory of the state
- Foreign civilian ships are allowed innocent passage through territorial waters
- Foreign ships are allowed transit passage through straits within territorial waters
- Sovereignty extends to the airspace above and the seabed below the territorial sea
- Adjustment of territorial water boundaries is called maritime delimitation in international law
