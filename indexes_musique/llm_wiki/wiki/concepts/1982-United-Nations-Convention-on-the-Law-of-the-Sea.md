# 1982 United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea
> An international treaty that establishes the legal definition of territorial waters and related maritime rights.

## Key Facts
- Provides the definition of territorial waters or territorial sea used in international law
- Established in 1982
- Specifies that territorial waters extend at most 12 nautical miles from a coastal state's baseline
