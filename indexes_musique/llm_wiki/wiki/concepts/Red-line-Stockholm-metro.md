# Red line (Stockholm metro)
> One of the lines of the Stockholm metro, whose first stretch opened in 1964 between T-Centralen and Fruängen.

## Key Facts
- Serves Gamla stan metro station.
- The first stretch of the Red line, between T-Centralen and Fruängen, opened on 5 April 1964.
