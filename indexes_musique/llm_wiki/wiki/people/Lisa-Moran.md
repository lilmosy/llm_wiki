# Lisa Moran
> Co-founder of Three Gut Records, an independent Guelph, Ontario record label.

## Key Facts
- Co-founded Three Gut Records in 1999 with Tyler Clarke Burke
- Started the label as a vehicle for releasing albums by their friends
