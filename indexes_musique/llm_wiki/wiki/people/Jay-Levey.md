# Jay Levey
> Co-writer of the film UHF, working alongside Weird Al Yankovic to bring musical parody comedy to the screen.

## Key Facts
- Co-wrote UHF with Weird Al Yankovic
- Collaborated on the concept of using a straight-man protagonist to enable parody sketches within the film
