# Avery James-Jennings
> One of the James-Jennings children and Stan's favorite, who knows his secret of talking.

## Key Facts
- One of the three James-Jennings children, along with Tyler and Chloe
- Knows that Stan can talk, along with Tyler, Chloe, Karl, and Grandma James
- Is Stan's favorite child, largely because they talk to each other the most
- Called Stan 'just a dog' in the episode 'Stan Runs Away', which offended him
- Along with Chloe and Tyler, discovers Stan's blog in 'The Kids Find Out Stan Blogs'
