# Francine Smith
> Fictional character in American Dad! who ends her marriage to Stan and becomes the bride of Jesus in 'Rapture's Delight'.

## Key Facts
- Mother of Steve Smith
- Mother of Hayley Smith
- Wife of Stan Smith in the series American Dad!.
- Ends her relationship with Stan after he blames her for not getting into heaven.
- Befriends a man whom she later discovers to be Jesus.
- Becomes Jesus's bride, leaving Stan behind to face the armageddon.
