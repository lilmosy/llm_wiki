# Jenny Kirtland
> Choir member of The Polyphonic Spree and co-founder of Kirtland Records alongside her husband John Kirtland.

## Key Facts
- Member of the choir for the band The Polyphonic Spree.
- Co-founded Kirtland Records in 2003 with her husband, John Kirtland.
