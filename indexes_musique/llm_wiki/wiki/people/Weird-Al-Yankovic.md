# Weird Al Yankovic
> Musician and comedian who co-wrote the film UHF, seeking to translate his musical parody style into film.

## Key Facts
- Co-wrote UHF with Jay Levey after releasing his second studio album
- Sought to apply his signature parody and comedy style to a film format
- Helped devise the concept of a straight-man protagonist with a vivid imagination to frame parody sketches
