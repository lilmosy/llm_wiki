# Gratia Countryman
> Chief librarian of Minneapolis Public Library who oversaw the 1931 relocation of the Linden Hills Library to its own building.

## Key Facts
- Served as chief librarian of Minneapolis Public Library
- Led the effort in 1931 to move the Linden Hills Library into its own building at 2900 West 43rd Street
