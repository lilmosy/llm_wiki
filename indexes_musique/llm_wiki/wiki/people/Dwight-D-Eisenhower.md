# Dwight D. Eisenhower
> 34th President of the United States, buried at the Eisenhower Presidential Library after a funeral train journey to Abilene, Kansas.

## Key Facts
- After his death, his body was placed on a funeral train to Abilene, Kansas—the last time a funeral train was used for an American president's funeral proceedings
- His body arrived in Abilene on April 2 and was interred that day in a small chapel on the grounds of the Eisenhower Presidential Library
- Was buried as a General of the Army
- The family used an $80 standard soldier's casket for his burial
- His body was dressed in his famous short green jacket
- Buried wearing only the Army Distinguished Service Medal with three oak leaf clusters, the Navy Distinguished Service Medal, and the Legion of Merit
- Is buried alongside his son Doud, who died at age 3 in 1921
- His wife Mamie was buried next to him after her death in 1979
