# Mike Medavoy
> American film producer and executive known for founding and leading several major film studios.

## Key Facts
- Full name Morris Mike Medavoy
- Born January 21, 1941
- American film producer and executive
- Co-founder of Orion Pictures in 1978
- Former chairman of TriStar Pictures
- Former head of production for United Artists from 1974 to 1978
- Current chairman and CEO of Phoenix Pictures
