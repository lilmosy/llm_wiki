# Stan (Dog with a Blog)
> Talking dog voiced by Stephen Full who belongs to the James-Jennings family and secretly blogs about his life.

## Key Facts
- Voiced by Stephen Full
- Is the James-Jennings family's dog who can blog and talk
- Keeps his ability to talk secret from Ellen and Bennett James-Jennings
- The only people who know he can talk are Avery, Tyler, Chloe, Karl, and Grandma James
- Hates noisy pomeranians and has a deep love of poodles
- Avery is shown to be his favorite of the three children, based on them talking most
- Enjoys the video game 'Realm of the Tower', as shown in the episode 'World of Woofcraft'
- Despite seeming human at times, retains many dog qualities and is sensitive about them
- Claims to be part cat, explaining that his dog ancestor 'married' a cat ancestor in the 1970s
- Has a beloved toy monkey named Robert who is his companion
- Panicked and exposed his secret to Grandma James when Robert was almost thrown away, causing her to faint
- Is very sensitive about being called 'just a dog', a label used by Avery in the episode 'Stan Runs Away'
- Secretly wrote a blog about his experiences with the family for a year before Avery, Chloe, and Tyler discovered it in 'The Kids Find Out Stan Blogs'
