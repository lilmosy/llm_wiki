# Mamie Eisenhower
> Wife of Dwight D. Eisenhower, buried next to him at the Eisenhower Presidential Library chapel after her death in 1979.

## Key Facts
- Died in 1979
- Was buried next to her husband Dwight D. Eisenhower after her death
- Interred in the small chapel on the grounds of the Eisenhower Presidential Library
