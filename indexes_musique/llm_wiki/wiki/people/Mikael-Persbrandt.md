# Mikael Persbrandt
> Swedish actor known for roles in the Beck film series, In a Better World, and The Hobbit.

## Key Facts
- Full name Mikael Åke Persbrandt, born 25 September 1963
- Best known in Sweden for playing Gunvald Larsson in the Beck series of films
- Starred in the Academy Award-winning film In a Better World, directed by Susanne Bier
- Received a 2011 European Film Award nomination for Best Actor for his role in In a Better World
- Played Carl Hamilton, a character from novels by Jan Guillou
- Played Beorn in The Hobbit film trilogy
