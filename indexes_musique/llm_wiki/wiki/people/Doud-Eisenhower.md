# Doud Eisenhower
> Son of Dwight D. Eisenhower who died at age 3 in 1921 and is buried at the Eisenhower Presidential Library chapel.

## Key Facts
- Died at age 3 in 1921
- Buried in the small chapel on the grounds of the Eisenhower Presidential Library
- His father Dwight D. Eisenhower is buried alongside him
