# Dian Bachar
> American actor born in Denver, Colorado, known for roles in films by Trey Parker and Matt Stone

## Key Facts
- Born October 26, 1970 in Denver, Colorado
- Played George Noon in 'Cannibal! The Musical'
- Played Ben Chapleski in 'Orgazmo'
- Played Kenny 'Squeak' Scolari in 'BASEketball' (1998), his most famous role
- Made occasional appearances on 'South Park'
- Appeared as an alien engineer in 'Galaxy Quest', though most of his scenes were cut and only visible in DVD Special Features
