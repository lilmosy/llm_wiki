# Ralph Rapson
> Architect who designed Riverside Plaza and the building later converted into the Southeast Library.

## Key Facts
- Designed Riverside Plaza (originally Cedar Square West), which opened in Minneapolis in 1973
- Designed the building at 1222 Fourth Street Southeast that originally served as the State Capitol Credit Union
- That building was later converted into the Southeast Library, opening in 1967
- That building was later converted into the Southeast Library, opening on December 26, 1967
