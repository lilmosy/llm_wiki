# George Washington
> President of the United States who dispatched a newly trained army to the Northwest Territory, leading to the decisive victory at Fallen Timbers.

## Key Facts
- As President, dispatched a newly trained army to the Northwest Territory in response to earlier U.S. defeats.
- This army decisively defeated the Indian confederacy at the Battle of Fallen Timbers in 1794.
