# Aidan Turner
> Irish actor known for roles in The Hobbit, Poldark, Desperate Romantics, The Clinic, and Being Human.

## Key Facts
- Born 19 June 1983
- Irish actor
- Played Kíli in the three-part fantasy film The Hobbit
- Played Ross Poldark in the 2015 BBC adaptation of the Poldark Novels by Winston Graham
- Played Dante Gabriel Rossetti in Desperate Romantics
- Played Ruairí McGowan in The Clinic
- Played John Mitchell in the supernatural drama series Being Human
