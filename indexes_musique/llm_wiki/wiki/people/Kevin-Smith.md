# Kevin Smith
> American filmmaker who founded SModcast Pictures and directed Red State

## Key Facts
- Founded SModcast Pictures in 2011
- Announced at the 2011 Sundance Film Festival that he would release his film 'Red State' himself
- Chose to distribute Red State via a tour rather than a traditional release
