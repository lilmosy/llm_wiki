# John Bell (Scottish actor)
> Scottish actor born 1997, known for roles in the Hobbit film series and other film and television productions.

## Key Facts
- Born October 20, 1997
- Scottish nationality
- Played Bain in two installments of the Hobbit film series
- Played Angus in Battleship
- Played Helius in Wrath of the Titans
- Played Toby Coleman in Tracy Beaker Returns
