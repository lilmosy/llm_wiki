# Steve Smith (American Dad!)
> Fictional character in American Dad! who is the teenage son of the Smith family.

## Key Facts
- Full name is Steven Anita Smith
- Voiced by Scott Grimes
- Only son of Stan and Francine Smith
- Younger brother of Hayley Smith
- The youngest of the show's six main characters
