# Barry Robinson
> Steve Smith's morbidly obese friend in American Dad! with an inarticulate voice and a hidden dark side.

## Key Facts
- Voiced by Eddie Kaye Thomas for the normal voice and Craig Ferguson for the evil voice
- Steve's morbidly obese friend with an inarticulate, strident, and sloppy vocal quality
- Stan disdains Barry, often impulsively yelling insults at him about his weight
- Like Snot, Barry can speak Klingon and seems able to read Quenya
- Has a fetish for Miss Piggy
- Borderline mentally retarded and innocent personality is the result of powerful anti-psychotic pills
- In one episode, after stopping his medication, Barry turns into a deranged and demonic mastermind speaking in a low-pitched voice like Gary Oldman
- Framed Steve for the destruction of Stan's beloved commemorative plates in an attempt to replace Steve within his immediate family
- Barry's madness grows worse over the episode, culminating in him trying to eliminate everyone between himself and Stan
- Steve ultimately tricks Barry into taking his medication, reverting him to his usual self
- Helped Snot and Toshi beat up a drugged Steve and screamed that they should kill Steve's whole family
- Describes his parents as ignorant and implies they engage in bondage sex play and smoke pot
- Statements about his parents imply they abuse him or are negligent, making him sleep in their basement and often forgetting he is their son
