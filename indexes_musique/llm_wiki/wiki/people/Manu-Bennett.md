# Manu Bennett
> New Zealand-Australian actor known for roles in epic fantasy television and film franchises.

## Key Facts
- Full name Jonathan Manu Bennett
- Born 10 October 1969
- Holds New Zealand and Australian nationality
- Primarily known for portraying characters in epic fantasy works
- Played Crixus in the TV series Spartacus
- Played Allanon in The Shannara Chronicles
- Played Slade Wilson / Deathstroke in Arrow
- Played Azog the Defiler in the Hobbit film trilogy
