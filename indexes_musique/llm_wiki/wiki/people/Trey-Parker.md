# Trey Parker
> Filmmaker and friend of Dian Bachar, involved in several films and shows Bachar appeared in

## Key Facts
- Voices four main South Park characters: Stan Marsh, Eric Cartman, Randy Marsh, and Mr. Garrison.
- Also voices several recurring characters including Clyde Donovan, Mr. Hankey, Mr. Mackey, Stephen Stotch, Jimmy Valmer, Timmy Burch, Tuong Lu Kim, and Phillip.
- Friend of actor Dian Bachar
- Associated with films and shows including 'Cannibal! The Musical', 'Orgazmo', 'BASEketball', and 'South Park'
