# Stan Smith
> Fictional character in American Dad! whose blame toward Francine after the Rapture leads to their breakup in 'Rapture's Delight'.

## Key Facts
- Father of Steve Smith
- Father of Hayley Smith
- Husband of Francine Smith in the series American Dad!.
- Blames Francine for not being raptured into heaven in the episode 'Rapture's Delight'.
- Left behind by Francine after she becomes Jesus's bride.
- Participates in the armageddon after being left by Francine.
