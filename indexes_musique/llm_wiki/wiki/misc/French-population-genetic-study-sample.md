# French population (genetic study sample)
> A European population sample used in Coop et al. (2009) to study allele frequency differentiation and selective sweeps.

## Key Facts
- Used as one of three reference populations (with Yoruba and Han) in Coop et al. (2009).
- Alleles differentiating the French from Yoruba and Han were analyzed for clinal or region-specific distribution across Europe.
- Associated with the 'west Eurasian sweeps' geographic pattern identified in the study.
