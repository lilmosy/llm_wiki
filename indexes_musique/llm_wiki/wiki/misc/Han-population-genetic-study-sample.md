# Han population (genetic study sample)
> An East Asian population sample used in Coop et al. (2009) to study selective sweeps distinct from European and African patterns.

## Key Facts
- Used as one of three reference populations (with French and Yoruba) in Coop et al. (2009).
- Alleles differentiating the Han from French and Yoruba were analyzed for geographic distribution patterns.
- Associated with the 'East Asian sweeps' geographic pattern identified in the study.
