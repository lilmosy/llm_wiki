# Salix arbuscula
> A low, much-branched shrub willow species found in Northern Europe, including parts of Scotland.

## Key Facts
- Common name is 'mountain willow'
- Grows as a low, much-branched shrub up to 0.7 metres tall
- Has a limited distribution in Northern Europe, occurring from north Scandinavia eastwards to Siberia
- In Scotland it grows on damp rocky mountain slopes and ledges
- In Scotland generally found at altitudes above 600 metres
- In Scotland it is rare outside Perthshire and Argyll
