# Robert (toy monkey)
> A toy monkey belonging to Stan the dog, treated as his beloved companion.

## Key Facts
- Belongs to Stan, who considers him his beloved companion
- Found by Grandma James while cleaning the family room
- Grandma James declared the toy so disgustingly filthy it was going straight to the trash
- Stan panicked and shouted Robert's name in front of Grandma James, exposing his secret ability to talk
- The incident caused Grandma James to faint
