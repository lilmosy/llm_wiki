# Yoruba population (genetic study sample)
> An African population sample used in Coop et al. (2009) as a reference group for identifying non-African selective sweeps.

## Key Facts
- Used as one of three reference populations (with French and Han) in Coop et al. (2009).
- Served as a comparison group to determine alleles differentiating French and Han populations.
- Associated with the 'non-African sweeps' geographic pattern identified in the study.
