# Spider-Man (comic book series)
> Comic book series created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko that inspired the 1967 animated TV series.

## Key Facts
- Created by writer Stan Lee and designed by artist Steve Ditko.
- Basis for the original 1967 animated television series.
