# Dog with a Blog
> Television series about the James-Jennings family and their talking, blogging dog Stan.

## Key Facts
- Features Stan, a talking dog who secretly blogs about the family's life
- Main characters include the James-Jennings family: Ellen, Bennett, Avery, Tyler, and Chloe
- Grandma James and Karl are also aware of Stan's secret ability to talk
- Episodes referenced include 'World of Woofcraft', 'Stan Runs Away', and 'The Kids Find Out Stan Blogs'
