# CFVS-DT
> French-language television station affiliated with V, licensed to Val-d'Or, Quebec, serving the Abitibi-Témiscamingue region

## Key Facts
- Branded on-air as V Abitibi-Témiscamingue
- Licensed to Val-d'Or, Quebec, Canada
- Broadcasts a digital signal on UHF channel 15 (virtual channel 25.1 via PSIP)
- Transmits from a location near Route Québec Lithium in La Corne
- Flash-cut to digital broadcasting on September 1, 2011
- Operates a rebroadcaster, CFVS-DT-1, in Rouyn-Noranda on UHF channel 20
