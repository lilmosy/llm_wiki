# WCCO-TV
> CBS owned-and-operated television station licensed to Minneapolis, Minnesota, serving the Twin Cities market

## Key Facts
- Broadcasts on virtual channel 4 (UHF digital channel 32)
- Licensed to Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States
- Serves the Twin Cities television market
- Owned by CBS Television Stations, a subsidiary of CBS Corporation
- Studios located on South 11th Street along Nicollet Mall in downtown Minneapolis
- Transmitter located at the Telefarm complex in Shoreview, Minnesota
