# UHF (film)
> Comedy film co-written by Weird Al Yankovic and Jay Levey, blending parody with a straight-man narrative device.

## Key Facts
- Written by Yankovic and Levey after Yankovic's second studio album
- Aimed to apply Yankovic's musical parody and comedy style to film
- Used the approach of protagonist George being a straight man with a vivid imagination to justify inclusion of parody segments
- Financing was difficult to secure; multiple production companies were approached before success
- Orion Pictures agreed to support the film after being told costs could be kept under $5 million
- Principal filming took place around Tulsa, Oklahoma
- Many film extras were drawn from the Tulsa and Dallas, Texas areas
