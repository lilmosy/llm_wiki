# Spider-Man (1967 TV series)
> The original animated TV series based on the Spider-Man comic book, airing 1967-1970.

## Key Facts
- Animated television series in the superhero fiction genre.
- Based on the Spider-Man comic book series created by Stan Lee and designed by Steve Ditko.
- Jointly produced in Canada (voice acting) and the United States (animation).
- First two seasons aired on the ABC television network; third season distributed in syndication.
- Grantray-Lawrence Animation produced the first season.
- Seasons two and three were produced by Krantz Films in New York City.
- Starred Paul Soles as Peter Parker (Spider-Man).
- Ran from September 9, 1967, to June 14, 1970.
