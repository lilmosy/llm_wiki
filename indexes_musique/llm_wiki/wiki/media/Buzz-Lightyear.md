# Buzz Lightyear
> Fictional space ranger action figure character central to the Toy Story franchise, voiced by different actors across media.

## Key Facts
- Voiced by Tim Allen in the Toy Story film series and the Buzz Lightyear movie
- Voiced by Patrick Warburton in the TV series
- Voiced by Pat Fraley in video games and Disney Parks attractions
