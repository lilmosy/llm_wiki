# In a Better World
> Academy Award-winning feature film directed by Susanne Bier, starring Mikael Persbrandt.

## Key Facts
- Won an Academy Award
- Directed by Susanne Bier
- Stars Mikael Persbrandt in a leading role
- Persbrandt's performance earned a 2011 European Film Award nomination for Best Actor
