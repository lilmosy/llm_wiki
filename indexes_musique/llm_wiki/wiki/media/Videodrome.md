# Videodrome
> 1983 Canadian science fiction body horror film about a UHF television executive drawn into a violent mind-control conspiracy.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1983
- Canadian science fiction body horror film
- Written and directed by David Cronenberg
- Stars James Woods, Sonja Smits, and Deborah Harry
- Set in Toronto during the early 1980s
- Follows the CEO of a small UHF television station who discovers a broadcast signal featuring extreme violence and torture
- Plot involves layers of deception and a mind-control conspiracy
- Protagonist loses touch with reality through increasingly bizarre and violent organic hallucinations
