# The Hobbit (South Park)
> Tenth and final episode of South Park's seventeenth season, featuring Kanye West's crusade over rumors about Kim Kardashian.

## Key Facts
- Tenth and final episode of the seventeenth season of South Park
- 247th episode of the series overall
- Premiered on Comedy Central in the United States on December 11, 2013
- Centers on Wendy Testaburger's attempts to raise awareness of media impact on body image
- Plot involves rapper Kanye West's crusade to convince the world his fiancée Kim Kardashian is not a hobbit
- Received largely positive reviews from critics, who praised Wendy's story arc and the return of Kanye West
