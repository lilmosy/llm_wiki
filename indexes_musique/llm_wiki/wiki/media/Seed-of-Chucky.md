# Seed of Chucky
> Horror film featuring an ensemble cast voicing and portraying Chucky, Tiffany, and various human characters.

## Key Facts
- Brad Dourif voices the character Chucky
- Jennifer Tilly appears as herself and voices the character Tiffany
- Billy Boyd voices the character Glen
- Beans El-Balawi portrays Human Glen
- Kristina Hewitt portrays Human Glenda
- Redman appears as himself
- Hannah Spearritt portrays the character Joan
- John Waters portrays the character Pete Peters
- Jason Flemyng appears as himself and portrays Santa
- Steve Lawton portrays the character Stan
- Tony Gardner appears as himself
- Rebecca Santos portrays the character Fulvia
- Keith-Lee Castle portrays the character Bill "Psychs" Sykes
- Paul Grossman portrays the character Little Boy
- Simon James Morgan portrays the character Richard
- Stephanie Chambers portrays the character Claudia's mom
- Betty Simons-Denville portrays the character Claudia
