# Le Mauricien
> French-language daily newspaper based and distributed in Mauritius, founded in 1908.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 1908
- Released daily
- One of the most read newspapers in Mauritius
- Independent newspaper
- Published by Le Mauricien Ltd.
