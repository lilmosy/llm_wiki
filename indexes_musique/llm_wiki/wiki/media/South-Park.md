# South Park
> Animated TV show created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone, with occasional appearances by Dian Bachar

## Key Facts
- American animated television series airing on Comedy Central
- Seventeenth season included the episode 'The Hobbit' as its tenth and final installment
- 'The Hobbit' was the 247th episode of the series overall
- Trey Parker voices main characters Stan Marsh, Eric Cartman, Randy Marsh, and Mr. Garrison.
- Trey Parker also voices recurring characters Clyde Donovan, Mr. Hankey, Mr. Mackey, Stephen Stotch, Jimmy Valmer, Timmy Burch, Tuong Lu Kim, and Phillip.
- Dian Bachar makes occasional appearances on the show
