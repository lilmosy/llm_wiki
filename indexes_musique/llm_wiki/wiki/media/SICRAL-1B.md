# SICRAL 1B
> Military communications satellite built for the Italian Armed Forces with dual-use civilian and military capacity.

## Key Facts
- Built by Thales Alenia Space for the Italian Armed Forces
- Dual-use spacecraft: Telespazio uses some transmission capacity, while the Italian defense ministry and NATO use the rest
- Based on the Italsat 3000 bus
- Includes one EHF/Ka band, three UHF-band and five active SHF-band transponders
- Designed to be operable for 13 years
