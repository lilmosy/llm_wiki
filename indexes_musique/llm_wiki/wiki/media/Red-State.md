# Red State
> Kevin Smith's film that was self-distributed under his company SModcast Pictures

## Key Facts
- Described as Kevin Smith's 'latest movie' as of the 2011 Sundance Film Festival
- Distributed by touring the film rather than through a traditional theatrical release
- Released under Kevin Smith's own distribution company, SModcast Pictures
