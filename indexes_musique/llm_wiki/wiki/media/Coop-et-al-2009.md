# Coop et al. (2009)
> A 2009 genetic study analyzing selected alleles differentiating French, Yoruba, and Han populations to identify patterns of selective sweeps.

## Key Facts
- Found that a selected allele strongly differentiating the French from both the Yoruba and Han could be clinal across Europe, restricted to Europe, or follow other geographic distributions depending on the nature of selective pressure.
- Determined that global geographic distributions of putatively selected alleles are largely explained by their frequencies in Yoruba, French, and Han populations (Figure 3 of the study).
- Identified three major geographic patterns of selection: non-African sweeps, west Eurasian sweeps, and East Asian sweeps.
