# Rapture's Delight
> Ninth episode of the sixth season of American Dad!, focusing on the aftermath of the Rapture for Stan and Francine.

## Key Facts
- Originally aired on Fox in the United States on December 13, 2009.
- Centers on Stan and Francine's life after most of the church, including Hayley and Steve, are raptured.
- Stan blames Francine for not being raptured into heaven, causing her to end their relationship.
- Francine befriends a man she later discovers to be Jesus.
- Francine becomes Jesus's bride, leaving Stan to participate in the armageddon.
