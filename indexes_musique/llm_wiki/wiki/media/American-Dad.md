# American Dad!
> American animated television series that includes the episode 'Rapture's Delight' in its sixth season.

## Key Facts
- Steve Smith is one of six main characters in the series
- Steve Smith is the son of Stan and Francine Smith and younger brother of Hayley Smith
- An American animated television series airing on Fox.
- Its sixth season includes the episode 'Rapture's Delight', which aired December 13, 2009.
