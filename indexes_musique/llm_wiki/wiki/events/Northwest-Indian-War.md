# Northwest Indian War
> Conflict between the United States and a confederacy of American Indian tribes in the Northwest Territory, sparked by U.S. settlement of lands ceded in the Treaty of Paris.

## Key Facts
- Provoked by U.S. settlement of lands in the Northwest Territory ceded by Britain in the Treaty of Paris without tribal consent.
- U.S. forces initially performed poorly in this war.
- Included the Battle of the Wabash in 1791, the worst defeat ever suffered by the United States at the hands of American Indians.
- Concluded in the region's favor for the U.S. after the Battle of Fallen Timbers in 1794, where a newly trained American army decisively defeated the Indian confederacy.
