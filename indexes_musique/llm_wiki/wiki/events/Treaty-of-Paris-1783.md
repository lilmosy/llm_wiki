# Treaty of Paris (1783)
> Post-Revolutionary War treaty in which Britain ceded lands between the Appalachians and the Mississippi to the United States without consulting the tribes living there.

## Key Facts
- Ceded lands between the Appalachian Mountains and the Mississippi River to the United States.
- Negotiated without consulting the Shawnee, Cherokee, Choctaw, and other smaller tribes who inhabited the ceded lands.
- Led the United States to compel tribal leaders to sign away lands in postwar treaties and begin dividing the lands for settlement.
