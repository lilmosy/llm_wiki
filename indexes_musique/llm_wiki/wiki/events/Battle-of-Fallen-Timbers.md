# Battle of Fallen Timbers
> 1794 battle in which a newly trained U.S. army under President Washington's direction decisively defeated the Indian confederacy in the Northwest Territory.

## Key Facts
- Fought in 1794 during the Northwest Indian War.
- President Washington dispatched a newly trained army to the region prior to this battle.
- The U.S. army decisively defeated the Indian confederacy in this battle.
