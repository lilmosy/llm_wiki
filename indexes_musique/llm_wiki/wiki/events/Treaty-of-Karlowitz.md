# Treaty of Karlowitz
> 1699 treaty by which the Ottoman Empire formally ceded the Kanije Eyalet to the Habsburg Monarchy.

## Key Facts
- Signed in 1699.
- Formally ceded the Kanije Eyalet to the Habsburg Monarchy, confirming the Habsburg capture of Kanije in 1690.
