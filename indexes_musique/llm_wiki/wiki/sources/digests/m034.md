# Not What He Seems (digest)

"Not What He Seems," the eleventh episode of Gravity Falls' second season, was written by Shion Takeuchi, Josh Weinstein, Jeff Rowe, Matt Chapman, and creator Alex Hirsch, and directed by Stephen Sandoval. The episode reveals Grunkle Stan's long-lost twin brother and ends on a cliffhanger, marking a major shift in the show's status quo.
