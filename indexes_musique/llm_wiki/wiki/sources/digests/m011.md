# UHF (film) (digest)

"Weird Al" Yankovic and Jay Levey co-wrote UHF following Yankovic's second album, designing the film around a straight-man protagonist to accommodate comedic parody sequences; after difficulty securing financing, Orion Pictures backed the project on the condition it stay under $5 million, and principal photography took place in the Tulsa, Oklahoma area with local extras drawn from Tulsa and Dallas.
