# CFVS-DT (digest)

CFVS-DT is a French-language television station affiliated with V, licensed to Val-d'Or, Quebec, that serves the Abitibi-Témiscamingue region and broadcasts on UHF channel 15 with a rebroadcaster (CFVS-DT-1) in Rouyn-Noranda on channel 20. Both transmitters converted to digital broadcasting on September 1, 2011.
