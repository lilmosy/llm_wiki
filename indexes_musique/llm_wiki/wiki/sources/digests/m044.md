# Wilkes Land (digest)

Wilkes Land is a ~2,600,000 km² sector of eastern Antarctica, stretching between Cape Hordern (100°31'E) and Pourquoi Pas Point (136°11'E) along the southern Indian Ocean and extending roughly 2600 km toward the South Pole. It is claimed by Australia as part of the Australian Antarctic Territory, though this claim's validity is suspended under the Antarctic Treaty.
