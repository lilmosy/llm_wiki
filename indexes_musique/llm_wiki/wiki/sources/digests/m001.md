# SICRAL 1B (digest)

SICRAL 1B is a dual-use military communications satellite built by Thales Alenia Space for the Italian Armed Forces, with capacity shared between Telespazio, the Italian defense ministry, and NATO. Built on the Italsat 3000 bus, it carries EHF/Ka, UHF, and SHF-band transponders and has a designed operational lifespan of 13 years.
