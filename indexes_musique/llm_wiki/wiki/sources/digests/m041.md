# Dwight D. Eisenhower (digest)

Dwight D. Eisenhower's body was transported by funeral train to Abilene, Kansas—the last such train used in a presidential funeral—and interred on April 2 at a chapel on the Eisenhower Presidential Library grounds, buried in a modest $80 casket with his signature green jacket and select military medals. He rests alongside his son Doud and, later, his wife Mamie.
