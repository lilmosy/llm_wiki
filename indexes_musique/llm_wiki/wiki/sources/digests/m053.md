# Northwest Territories (digest)

The Northwest Territories, a Canadian territory spanning 1,183,085 km² in northern Canada, borders Yukon, Nunavut, British Columbia, Alberta, and Saskatchewan (with a possible quadripoint near Manitoba), and features major geographical landmarks including Great Bear Lake, Great Slave Lake, the Mackenzie River, Nahanni National Park Reserve, several Arctic islands, and its highest point, Mount Nirvana (2,773 m).
