# Dian Bachar (digest)

Dian Bachar is an American actor born October 26, 1970, in Denver, Colorado, best known for collaborations with Trey Parker and Matt Stone in films like "Cannibal! The Musical," "Orgazmo," and "BASEketball" (as Kenny "Squeak" Scolari), as well as occasional "South Park" appearances. He also had a mostly cut role as an alien engineer in "Galaxy Quest," visible in the DVD's special features.
