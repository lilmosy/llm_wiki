# Common nightingale (digest)

The common nightingale is a migratory, insectivorous songbird that breeds in forests and scrublands across Europe and southwest Asia, wintering in sub-Saharan Africa, and is not native to the Americas. It typically nests near the ground in dense vegetation, with research in Germany identifying specific geographical factors that define its preferred breeding habitat, distinguishing it from the more northerly-distributed thrush nightingale.
