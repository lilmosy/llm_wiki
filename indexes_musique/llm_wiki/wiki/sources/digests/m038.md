# Spider-Man (1967 TV series) (digest)

The 1967 "Spider-Man" animated TV series, the first adaptation of Stan Lee and Steve Ditko's comic, aired on ABC and later in syndication from 1967 to 1970. Produced across Canada and the U.S. (first by Grantray-Lawrence Animation, then Krantz Films), it starred Paul Soles as the voice of Peter Parker/Spider-Man.
