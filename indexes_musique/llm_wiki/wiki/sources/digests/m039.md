# Aidan Turner (digest)

Aidan Turner (born June 19, 1983) is an Irish actor best known for playing Kíli in The Hobbit film trilogy and Ross Poldark in the 2015 BBC adaptation of the Poldark novels. His other notable television roles include Dante Gabriel Rossetti in Desperate Romantics, Ruairí McGowan in The Clinic, and John Mitchell in Being Human.
