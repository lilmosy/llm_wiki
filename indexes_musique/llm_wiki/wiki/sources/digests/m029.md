# Gemini Division (digest)

Gemini Division is an American sci-fi web series created by Electric Farm Entertainment, produced starting March 2008 and airing on NBC.com from August 18, 2008. Based on a story by Brent Friedman and developed by Joshua Stern, it stars Rosario Dawson and involves showrunners J Alex Reed and Duane Loose, with Stan Rogow and Neal Israel directing episodes.
