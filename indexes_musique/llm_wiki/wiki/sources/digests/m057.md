# Louisiana Purchase (digest)

Spain revoked and then restored American rights to New Orleans around 1798-1801, but had secretly ceded Louisiana back to France in 1800, leading to a brief Spanish-to-French transfer of power in November 1803 followed by the formal cession to the United States in December 1803. A delayed ceremony in St. Louis in March 1804, known as Three Flags Day, marked the transition for Upper Louisiana.
