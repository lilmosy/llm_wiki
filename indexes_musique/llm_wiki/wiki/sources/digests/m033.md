# Richard Armitage (actor) (digest)

Richard Armitage (b. 1971) is an English actor known for his early UK breakthrough as John Thornton in *North & South* (2004) and his internationally recognized role as Thorin Oakenshield in Peter Jackson's *The Hobbit* trilogy. Other notable credits include *Hannibal*, *Spooks*, *Strike Back*, *Robin Hood*, and voicing Trevor Belmont in Netflix's *Castlevania*.
