# Military history of the United States (digest)

After the American Revolution, the U.S. gained lands between the Appalachians and the Mississippi River without tribal consent, prompting Native nations to be coerced into treaties as settlers moved in. This led to conflict in the Northwest Territory, including a major U.S. defeat at the Battle of the Wabash (1791), before American forces decisively won at the Battle of Fallen Timbers (1794).
