# Dog with a Blog (digest)

Stan is a talking dog on "Dog with a Blog" who secretly blogs about his life with the James-Jennings family, keeping his talking ability hidden from the parents while several family members and friends know his secret. He has distinct personality traits—including a favoritism toward Avery, a love of poodles, sensitivity about being called "just a dog," and a treasured toy monkey named Robert—that occasionally lead to close calls exposing his secret.
