# The Hobbit (South Park) (digest)

"The Hobbit" is the 247th episode of South Park and the season 17 finale, which aired December 11, 2013. The episode follows Wendy Testaburger's campaign against media-driven body image standards alongside Kanye West's parallel crusade to prove Kim Kardashian isn't a hobbit, earning positive critical reception for Wendy's storyline and West's return.
