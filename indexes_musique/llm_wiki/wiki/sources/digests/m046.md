# Kanije Eyalet (digest)

The Kanije Eyalet was an Ottoman province established in 1600 following the capture of Kanije from the Habsburgs, formed by merging this territory with the Zigetvar Province (itself created in 1596 from parts of Budin and Bosnia Provinces). It existed until 1690, when Kanije was recaptured by the Habsburg Monarchy, with the territory formally ceded via the 1699 Treaty of Karlowitz.
