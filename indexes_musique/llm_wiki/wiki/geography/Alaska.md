# Alaska
> U.S. state bordered by the Pacific Ocean, Bering Strait, and Arctic Ocean.

## Key Facts
- Alaska borders the Pacific Ocean to the south.
- Alaska borders the Bering Strait to the west.
- Alaska borders the Arctic Ocean to the north.
