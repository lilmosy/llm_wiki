# Clerke Rocks
> A group of small rocky islands southeast of South Georgia, extending east to west.

## Key Facts
- Located some distance southeast of South Georgia
- Extends from east to west as a chain of small rocky islands
- Includes The Office Boys at the northeastern end and Nobby at the southeastern end
- Administered as part of the British Overseas Territory of South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands
- Claimed by Argentina as part of Tierra del Fuego Province
