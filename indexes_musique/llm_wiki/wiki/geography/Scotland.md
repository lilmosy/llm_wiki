# Scotland
> A country in the UK where Salix arbuscula grows on damp rocky mountain slopes, mainly in Perthshire and Argyll.

## Key Facts
- Habitat for Salix arbuscula on damp rocky mountain slopes and ledges
- Salix arbuscula is generally found there at altitudes above 600 metres
- Salix arbuscula is rare outside Perthshire and Argyll within Scotland
