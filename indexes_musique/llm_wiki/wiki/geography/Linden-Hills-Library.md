# Linden Hills Library
> A public library branch in the Linden Hills neighborhood of southwest Minneapolis, Minnesota, notable for its Tudor Revival building and historic designation.

## Key Facts
- Located in the Linden Hills neighborhood of southwest Minneapolis, Minnesota, United States
- Originally opened in 1911 on the first floor of the Lake Harriet Commercial Club building
- In 1931, moved into its own building at 2900 West 43rd Street under the leadership of chief librarian Gratia Countryman
- Designed by area resident Joseph Victor Vanderbilt in the Tudor Revival style
- Head librarian Edith Frost served the library for over thirty years
- Has hosted community groups including children's clubs, neighborhood groups, and women's organizations
- Listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2000
- Renovated in 2002
