# Northwest Territories
> A territory in northern Canada bordered by Yukon, Nunavut, and three provinces, containing major lakes, a river, and Arctic islands.

## Key Facts
- Located in northern Canada
- Borders Yukon to the west and Nunavut to the east
- Borders British Columbia to the southwest, and Alberta and Saskatchewan to the south
- Possibly meets Manitoba at an unsurveyed quadripoint to the extreme southeast
- Has a land area of 1,183,085 km2 (456,792 sq mi)
- Contains Great Bear Lake, the largest lake entirely within Canada
- Contains Great Slave Lake, the deepest body of water in North America at 614 m (2,014 ft)
- The Mackenzie River flows through the territory
- Contains the canyons of the Nahanni National Park Reserve, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
- Includes territorial islands in the Canadian Arctic Archipelago: Banks Island, Borden Island, Prince Patrick Island, and parts of Victoria Island and Melville Island
- Highest point is Mount Nirvana near the Yukon border at 2,773 m (9,098 ft)
