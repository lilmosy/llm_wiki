# Pimpirev Glacier
> A glacier on Livingston Island in the South Shetland Islands, Antarctica, draining southeastwards toward Pimpirev Beach.

## Key Facts
- Located on Livingston Island in the South Shetland Islands, Antarctica.
- Situated south of the glacial divide between the Drake Passage and Bransfield Strait.
- Positioned southeast of Tundzha Glacier, southwest of Saedinenie Snowfield, west of Perunika Glacier, and east-northeast of Kamchiya Glacier.
- Extends 5.5 km in a southeast-northwest direction and 1.8 km in a northwest-southeast direction.
- Drains southeastwards towards Pimpirev Beach.
- Mostly terminates on the shore, but on several occasions penetrates the South Bay waters east-northeast of Ereby Point.
