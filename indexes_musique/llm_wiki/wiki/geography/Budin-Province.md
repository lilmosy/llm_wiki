# Budin-Province
> Ottoman province that was expanded during the Long War and contributed sanjaks to the formation of Zigetvar Province in 1596.

## Key Facts
- Was expanded as a result of Ottoman territorial gains during the Long War.
- Contributed some of its sanjaks toward the formation of Zigetvar Province in 1596.
