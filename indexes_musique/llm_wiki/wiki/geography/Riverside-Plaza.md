# Riverside Plaza
> A modernist and brutalist apartment complex in Minneapolis designed by Ralph Rapson, opened in 1973.

## Key Facts
- Designed by Ralph Rapson
- Opened in Minneapolis, Minnesota in 1973
- Located on the edge of downtown Minneapolis in the Cedar-Riverside neighborhood
- Situated next to the University of Minnesota's West Bank and Augsburg University
- Contains the 39-story McKnight Building, the tallest structure outside Minneapolis's central business district
- Initially known as Cedar Square West
- Exterior shots of the complex were featured on television as the residence of Mary Richards in the sixth and seventh seasons of 'The Mary Tyler Moore Show'
