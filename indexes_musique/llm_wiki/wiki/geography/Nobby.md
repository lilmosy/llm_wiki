# Nobby
> A small islet forming the southeastern end of the Clerke Rocks group, known in Spanish as Islote Llamativo or Roca Notable.

## Key Facts
- Located at the southeastern end of the Clerke Rocks
- Known in Spanish as 'Islote Llamativo' or 'Roca Notable'
- Part of the group of small rocky islands southeast of South Georgia
