# Mississippi River
> Major river that flows through Minneapolis, just north of its confluence with the Minnesota River.

## Key Facts
- Minneapolis lies on both of its banks
- Its confluence with the Minnesota River is just south of Minneapolis
- Contributes to Minneapolis's abundant water features alongside lakes, wetlands, creeks, and waterfalls
