# Gamla stan metro station
> A Stockholm metro station located in the Gamla stan district, served by the Red and Green lines.

## Key Facts
- Located in the district of Gamla stan.
- Served by both the Red line and the Green line of the Stockholm metro.
- Opened on 24 November 1957 as part of the connection between Slussen and Hötorget, which connected the east and west parts of the Green line.
