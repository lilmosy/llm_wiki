# Cedar-Riverside
> A neighborhood on the edge of downtown Minneapolis, home to Riverside Plaza.

## Key Facts
- Located on the edge of downtown Minneapolis
- Contains the Riverside Plaza apartment complex
- Adjacent to the University of Minnesota's West Bank and Augsburg University
