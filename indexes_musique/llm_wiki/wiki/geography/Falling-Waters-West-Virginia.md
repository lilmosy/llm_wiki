# Falling Waters, West Virginia
> A census-designated place on the Potomac River in Berkeley County, West Virginia, with a 2010 population of 876.

## Key Facts
- Located on the Potomac River in Berkeley County, West Virginia
- Situated along Williamsport Pike (US 11) north of Martinsburg
- Had a population of 876 according to the 2010 census
- An 1887 'Scientific American' article claimed the first U.S. railroad was built here in 1814
