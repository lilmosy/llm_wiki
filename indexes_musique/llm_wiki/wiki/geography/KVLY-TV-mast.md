# KVLY-TV mast
> A guyed mast in North Dakota that was the tallest structure in the world and is used for VHF-UHF television transmission

## Key Facts
- Stands 628.8 m (2,063 ft) tall
- Completed in 1963
- Type: guyed mast
- Used for VHF-UHF transmission
- Located in Blanchard, North Dakota, United States
- Coordinates: 47°20′31.85″N 97°17′21.13″W
- Noted as the tallest mast in the world
