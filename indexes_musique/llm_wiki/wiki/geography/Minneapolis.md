# Minneapolis
> Minnesota city on the Mississippi River, historically a flour milling and timber hub, now a major regional business center.

## Key Facts
- Lies on both banks of the Mississippi River, just north of its confluence with the Minnesota River
- Adjoins Saint Paul, the capital of Minnesota
- Rich in water features: 13 lakes, wetlands, the Mississippi River, creeks, and waterfalls, many connected by parkways in the Chain of Lakes and the Grand Rounds National Scenic Byway
- Once the world's flour milling capital and a hub for timber
- The city and surrounding region is the primary business center between Chicago and Seattle
- As of 2018, home to 6 Fortune 500 companies
- Part of the Twin Cities, the fifth-largest hub of major corporate headquarters in the United States as of 2018
- Categorized as a global city due to its integral link to the global economy
