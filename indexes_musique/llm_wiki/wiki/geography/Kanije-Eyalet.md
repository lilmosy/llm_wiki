# Kanije Eyalet
> Ottoman province established in 1600 around the captured town of Kanije, existing until 1690 and formally ceded to the Habsburgs in 1699.

## Key Facts
- Established in 1600 after the town of Kanije was captured from the Habsburgs.
- Formed by joining the newly conquered Kanije territory with the territory of Zigetvar Province.
- Existed until 1690, when Kanije was captured by the Habsburg Monarchy.
- Formally ceded to the Habsburg Monarchy by the Treaty of Karlowitz in 1699.
- Existed until 1690, when Kanije was recaptured by the Habsburg Monarchy.
- Formally ceded to the Habsburg Monarchy via the Treaty of Karlowitz in 1699.
