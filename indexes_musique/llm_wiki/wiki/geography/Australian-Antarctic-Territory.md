# Australian Antarctic Territory
> A territory in Antarctica claimed by Australia, of which Wilkes Land forms a part.

## Key Facts
- Includes Wilkes Land as a formally claimed district
- Claimed by Australia
- Validity of Australia's claim is placed in abeyance during the operation of the Antarctic Treaty
