# McKnight Building
> A 39-story building within Riverside Plaza, the tallest structure outside Minneapolis's central business district.

## Key Facts
- Stands 39 stories tall
- Part of the Riverside Plaza apartment complex
- The tallest structure outside of Minneapolis's central business district
