# Geography of the United States
> Overview of the United States' land and territorial water borders with neighboring countries and surrounding bodies of water.

## Key Facts
- The United States shares a land border with Canada to the north.
- The United States shares a land border with Mexico to the south.
- The United States shares a territorial water border with Russia in the northwest.
- The United States has a territorial water border between Florida and Cuba in the southeast.
- The United States has a territorial water border between Florida and the Bahamas in the southeast.
- The contiguous forty-eight states are bounded by the Pacific Ocean on the west.
- The contiguous forty-eight states are bounded by the Atlantic Ocean on the east.
- The contiguous forty-eight states are bounded by the Gulf of Mexico to the southeast.
- Alaska borders the Pacific Ocean to the south, the Bering Strait to the west, and the Arctic Ocean to the north.
- Hawaii lies far to the southwest of the mainland in the Pacific Ocean.
