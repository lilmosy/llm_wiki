# Zigetvar Province
> Ottoman province formed in 1596 from sanjaks of Budin Province and Bosnia Province, later merged into Kanije Eyalet.

## Key Facts
- Formed in 1596 from some sanjaks of Budin Province and Bosnia Province.
- Budin Province had been expanded due to Ottoman territorial gains during the Long War prior to this reorganization.
- Its territory was joined with the newly conquered area of Kanije in 1600 to establish the Kanije Eyalet.
