# Slussen
> A location in Stockholm that served as one endpoint of the 1957 Green line connection through Gamla stan.

## Key Facts
- Was one endpoint of the stretch opened on 24 November 1957 that connected to Hötorget via Gamla stan, joining the east and west parts of the Green line.
