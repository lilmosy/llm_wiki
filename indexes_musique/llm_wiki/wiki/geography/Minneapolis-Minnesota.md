# Minneapolis, Minnesota
> City in Minnesota, United States, that is the licensed home of WCCO-TV and site of its studios

## Key Facts
- WCCO-TV is licensed to Minneapolis, Minnesota
- WCCO-TV's studios are located on South 11th Street along Nicollet Mall in downtown Minneapolis
