# Wilkes Land
> A large district of land in eastern Antarctica claimed by Australia, fronting the southern Indian Ocean.

## Key Facts
- Formally claimed by Australia as part of the Australian Antarctic Territory
- Validity of the claim has been placed in abeyance for the period of operation of the Antarctic Treaty, to which Australia is a signatory
- Fronts on the southern Indian Ocean between Queen Mary Coast and Adelie Land
- Extends from Cape Hordern at 100°31' E to Pourquoi Pas Point at 136°11' E
- Extends as a sector about 2600 km towards the South Pole
- Has an estimated land area of 2,600,000 km², mostly glaciated
- Is subdivided into coastal areas that can be thought of as sectors extending to the South Pole
