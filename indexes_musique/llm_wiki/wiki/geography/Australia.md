# Australia
> A nation that claims Wilkes Land as part of the Australian Antarctic Territory and is a signatory to the Antarctic Treaty.

## Key Facts
- Formally claims Wilkes Land as part of the Australian Antarctic Territory
- Is a signatory to the Antarctic Treaty
- Claims Wilkes Land as part of the Australian Antarctic Territory
