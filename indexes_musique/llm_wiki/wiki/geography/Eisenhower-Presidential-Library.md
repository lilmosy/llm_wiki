# Eisenhower Presidential Library
> Presidential library complex in Abilene, Kansas, containing the small chapel where Dwight D. Eisenhower and family members are interred.

## Key Facts
- Located in Abilene, Kansas
- Contains a small chapel on its grounds where Eisenhower was interred on April 2 after his death
- Serves as the burial site for Dwight D. Eisenhower, his son Doud, and his wife Mamie
