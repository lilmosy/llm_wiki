# Italian Armed Forces
> Military organization of Italy for which the SICRAL 1B satellite was built.

## Key Facts
- Commissioned SICRAL 1B, built by Thales Alenia Space
- Shares satellite capacity with Telespazio, the Italian defense ministry, and NATO
