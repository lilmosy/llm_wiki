# Orion Pictures
> American film production company co-founded in 1978, including by Mike Medavoy.

## Key Facts
- Provided financing support for UHF after the filmmakers struggled to find backing
- Agreed to fund the film based on assurance that costs could be kept under $5 million
- Founded in 1978
- Co-founded by Mike Medavoy
