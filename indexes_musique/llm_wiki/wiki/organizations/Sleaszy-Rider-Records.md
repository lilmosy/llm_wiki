# Sleaszy Rider Records
> Independent Greek record label founded in 1999 that also distributes releases from other international labels.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 1999 by Tolis G. Palantzas
- Head office located in Greece
- Mainly distributed in Europe by Sony Music/EMI
- Distributes releases in Greece from numerous labels, including Roadrunner Records, SPV, and Pagan Records
