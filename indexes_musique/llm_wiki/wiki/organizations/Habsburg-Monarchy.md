# Habsburg Monarchy
> European power that lost and later recaptured Kanije from the Ottomans, formalizing the acquisition of Kanije Eyalet via the Treaty of Karlowitz.

## Key Facts
- Lost the town of Kanije to the Ottomans in 1600, prompting the creation of Kanije Eyalet.
- Recaptured Kanije from the Ottomans in 1690.
- Received formal cession of Kanije Eyalet through the Treaty of Karlowitz in 1699.
