# Kirtland Records
> Independent record label co-founded in 2003 by John Kirtland and Jenny Kirtland, home to several Texas-based indie bands.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 2003 by John Kirtland and his wife Jenny Kirtland.
- Leveraged the back catalog rights of the band Bush to secure a national distribution deal with RED Distribution.
- RED Distribution remains the label's distributor.
- Became the record label for a number of Texas-based indie bands including Burden Brothers, The Polyphonic Spree, Toadies, The Vanished, and Sarah Jaffe.
