# SModcast Pictures
> American film distribution and production company founded by Kevin Smith in 2011

## Key Facts
- Founded in 2011 by Kevin Smith
- Functions as both a film and television production company and a film distribution company
- Used to self-distribute Kevin Smith's film 'Red State' by touring it instead of a traditional theatrical release
