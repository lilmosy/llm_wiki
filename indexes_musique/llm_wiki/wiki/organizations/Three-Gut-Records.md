# Three Gut Records
> Independent record label based in Guelph, Ontario, Canada, founded in 1999.

## Key Facts
- Based in Guelph, Ontario, Canada
- Founded in 1999 by Lisa Moran and Tyler Clarke Burke as a vehicle for releasing albums by their friends
- Became an influential player in Canadian music following the breakout success of the Constantines' self-titled 2001 release
- Releases were distributed by Outside Music
