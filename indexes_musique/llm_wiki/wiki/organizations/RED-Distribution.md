# RED Distribution
> National distribution company that entered a distribution deal with Kirtland Records, which it continues to serve.

## Key Facts
- Signed a national distribution deal with Kirtland Records, secured via the back catalog rights of the band Bush.
- Remains the distributor for Kirtland Records.
