# ADI (Airliners Distributing Inc.)
> Founding company behind the GeminiJets diecast model product line, holding production licenses from major airlines and aircraft manufacturers.

## Key Facts
- Holds production licenses from airlines and aircraft manufacturers including Boeing, Airbus, McDonnell Douglas, and Embraer
- Makes and markets diecast aircraft models in several different scales
- Is the founding company of the GeminiJets product line
