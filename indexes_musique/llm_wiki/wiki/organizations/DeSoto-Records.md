# DeSoto Records
> American independent record label based in Washington, D.C., founded in 1989.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 1989
- Based in Washington, D.C.
- Run by Bill Barbot and Kim Coletta, both formerly of the band Jawbox
- Originally founded by members of the band Edsel to release their first single, "My Manacles"
- Jawbox later used the name DeSoto for their first EP
- Has released over 40 records, including 7" singles, CDs, and LP vinyl records
- Distributed by Fontana Distribution
