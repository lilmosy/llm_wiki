# Marketwired
> Press release distribution service headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 1993
- Incorporated in the United States in 1999
- Distributes press releases via traditional methods such as Associated Press and Bloomberg
- Also offers new media distribution methods and other related services
- Headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, Canada
