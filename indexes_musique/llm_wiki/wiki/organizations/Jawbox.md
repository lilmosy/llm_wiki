# Jawbox
> Band whose former members Bill Barbot and Kim Coletta later ran DeSoto Records, and which released its first EP under the DeSoto name.

## Key Facts
- Former members include Bill Barbot and Kim Coletta
- Used the name DeSoto for their first EP
- Members later ran DeSoto Records, though it was originally founded by members of the band Edsel
