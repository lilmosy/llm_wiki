# GeminiJets
> Diecast model airplane product line based in Las Vegas, Nevada, founded in 1998 under the name SkyJets.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 1998 originally under the name SkyJets
- Based in Las Vegas, Nevada, United States
- Is a product line name; the founding company is ADI (Airliners Distributing Inc.)
