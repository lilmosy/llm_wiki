# State Capitol Credit Union
> A credit union for university and state employees whose building at 1222 Fourth Street Southeast was later converted into the Southeast Library.

## Key Facts
- Served university and state employees
- Occupied a building at 1222 Fourth Street Southeast designed by architect Ralph Rapson
- The building was purchased for conversion into a library on December 29, 1966
- The building reopened as the Southeast Library on December 26, 1967
