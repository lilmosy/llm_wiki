# Amblin Entertainment
> American film production company founded in 1981 by Steven Spielberg, Kathleen Kennedy, and Frank Marshall.

## Key Facts
- Founded in 1981
- Headquarters located on the backlot of Universal Studios in Universal City, California
- Distributes all films from Amblin Partners under the Amblin Entertainment name
