# House of Ascania
> German noble dynasty to which the princes of Anhalt-Zerbst, including Albert I, Albert II, Bernhard VII, John V, Rudolph, and John VI, belonged.

## Key Facts
- Was the dynasty of Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the dynasty of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the dynasty of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the dynasty of Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the dynasty of Rudolph, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the dynasty of John VI, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the dynasty of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
