# SIFAL
> Argentine production company run by Alberto De Zavalia and Luis Saslavsky that produced Escala en la ciudad.

## Key Facts
- Production company run by Alberto De Zavalia and his partner Luis Saslavsky
- Produced the film Escala en la ciudad
- Disbanded the year after Escala en la ciudad was made, following completion of Luis Saslavsky's film Crimen a las tres
