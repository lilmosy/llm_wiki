# Eni
> Italian state-owned oil company developed by Enrico Mattei after World War II.

## Key Facts
- Formed from Italy's nascent oil and hydrocarbon industry after World War II
- Developed by Enrico Mattei to avoid its sale to US companies
- Rivaled the 'seven sisters' for oil and gas deals in North Africa and the Middle East
