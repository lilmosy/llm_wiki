# Township High School District 214
> A school district in Illinois that includes Wheeling High School and five other high schools.

## Key Facts
- Includes Wheeling High School, Buffalo Grove High School, Elk Grove High School, John Hersey High School, Prospect High School, and Rolling Meadows High School
- Wheeling High School was ranked 6th among the district's high schools by U.S. News & World Report in 2019
