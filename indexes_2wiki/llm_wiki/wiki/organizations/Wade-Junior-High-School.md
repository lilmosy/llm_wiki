# Wade Junior High School
> Junior high school in the Bronx, New York, where Etan Boritzer wrote his first published essay in 1963.

## Key Facts
- Located in the Bronx, New York
- Etan Boritzer wrote an essay in his English class here in 1963 at age 13, on the assassination of John F. Kennedy
- That essay was Boritzer's first published work
