# House of La Trémoille
> French noble family to which Marie Charlotte de La Trémoille and her father Henri III belonged

## Key Facts
- Included Henri III de La Trémoille, 3rd Duke of Thouars, as a prominent member
- Marie Charlotte de La Trémoille was a member of this house by birth
