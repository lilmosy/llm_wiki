# Filmation
> An animation production company that produced 'A Snow White Christmas' and later 'Happily Ever After', both sequels to 'Snow White'.

## Key Facts
- Produced the 1980 special 'A Snow White Christmas'
- Also produced 'Happily Ever After' (1990), another unrelated sequel to 'Snow White'
