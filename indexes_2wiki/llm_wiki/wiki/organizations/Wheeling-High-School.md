# Wheeling High School
> A public four-year high school in Wheeling, Illinois, part of Township High School District 214.

## Key Facts
- Located in Wheeling, Illinois, a northwest suburb of Chicago, Illinois, United States
- Serves the communities of Wheeling, Prospect Heights, Arlington Heights, Buffalo Grove, and Mount Prospect
- Ranked 6th among high schools in Township High School District 214 by U.S. News & World Report in 2019
- Ranked 90th among high schools in Illinois by U.S. News & World Report in 2019
- Ranked 2,448th among high schools in the United States by U.S. News & World Report in 2019
