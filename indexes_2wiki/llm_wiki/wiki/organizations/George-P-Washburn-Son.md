# George P. Washburn & Son
> Architectural firm that designed the Ottawa High School building and later, as Washburn & Stookey, designed the adjoining junior high school in the same style.

## Key Facts
- Designed the Ottawa High School building in the Collegiate Gothic style, built in 1917
- By the time the junior high school was constructed ten years later, the firm was known as Washburn & Stookey
- Washburn & Stookey designed the junior high school building in the same Collegiate Gothic style as the high school
