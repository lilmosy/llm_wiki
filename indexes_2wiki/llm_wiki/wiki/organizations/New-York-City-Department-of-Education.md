# New York City Department of Education
> New York City government agency that compiled and published a 1963 anthology of essays by public school children, including Etan Boritzer's essay.

## Key Facts
- Compiled and published a special anthology of essays by New York City public school children in 1963
- The anthology included Etan Boritzer's essay on the assassination of John F. Kennedy, his first published work
