# North Marion High School
> Public high school in Aurora, Oregon, part of the North Marion School District

## Key Facts
- Public Double A ("AA") high school in the U.S. state of West Virginia
- Current enrollment of 851 students
- Located approximately 4 miles from Farmington, West Virginia on US Route 250 north
- Closer to Mannington, West Virginia than to Farmington, but uses Farmington as its mailing address
- Often considered to be located in Rachel, West Virginia
- Represented as "Grantville High School" in the novel "1632" by Eric Flint
- Rachel, West Virginia is a small coal mining community adjacent to the school
- Public high school located in Aurora, Oregon, United States
- Part of the North Marion School District
- All four schools in the district, including this one, are located on the same campus
- Draws students from the cities of Aurora, Hubbard, and Donald
- Draws students from the communities of Broadacres and Butteville
