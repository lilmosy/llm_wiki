# East High School (Denver)
> Public high school in Denver's City Park neighborhood and one of the district's four original high schools.

## Key Facts
- Located in the City Park neighborhood on the east side of Denver, Colorado, United States.
- Part of the Denver Public Schools system.
- One of four original high schools established in Denver, alongside North, West, and South High Schools.
