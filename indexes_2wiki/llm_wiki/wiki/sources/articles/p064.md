# Louis Charles de La Trémoille

Prince Louis Charles de La Trémoille( 26 October 1838 – 4 July 1911), 9th Duke of Thouars, 8th Duke of La Trémoïlle, 11th prince de Tarente, 15th prince de Talmond and 15th Count of Laval was a French aristocrat and the son of Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille and his third wife, Valentine Eugénie Joséphine Walsh de Serrant. Louis Charles was also an archivist, bibliophile and also collector.
