# Liberty Ross

Liberty Lettice Lark Ross( born 23 September 1978) is an English model and actress. She has appeared in publications such as" VogueHarper's Bazaari- D", and" Dazed& Confused". She played the role of Queen Eleanor in the 2012 fantasy film" Snow White and the Huntsman", directed by her then- husband, Rupert Sanders. She is the sister of composers Atticus and Leopold Ross.
