# Escala en la ciudad

Escala en la ciudad(" A stop in the city") is a Argentine film directed and written by Alberto De Zavalia with Carlos Aden. It was produced by SIFAL, a production company run by de Zavalia and his partner Luis Saslavsky. The production company disbanded the following year, after de Zavalia had made" Escala en la ciudad", his feature film debut, and Saslavsky had completed his second and most famous movie," Crimen a las tres".
