# Rafaela Aparicio

Rafaela Díaz Valiente( 9 April 1906 – 9 June 1996) better known as Rafaela Aparicio was a famous Spanish film and theatre actress. She made more than 100 films. The most remembered are Carlos Saura's" Anna and the WolvesMama Turns 100" and Fernando Fernán Gómez's" El extraño viaje". She died of a stroke in Madrid in a retirement home.
