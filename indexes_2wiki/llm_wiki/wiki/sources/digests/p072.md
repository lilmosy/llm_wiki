# Wheeling High School (digest)

Wheeling High School (WHS) is a public four-year high school in Wheeling, Illinois, part of Township High School District 214, serving communities including Wheeling, Prospect Heights, Arlington Heights, Buffalo Grove, and Mount Prospect. In 2019, U.S. News & World Report ranked it 6th within its district, 90th in Illinois, and 2,448th nationally.
