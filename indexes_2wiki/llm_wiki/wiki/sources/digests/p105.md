# Escala en la ciudad (digest)

"Escala en la ciudad" ("A Stop in the City") is an Argentine film written and directed by Alberto de Zavalía with Carlos Adén, produced by SIFAL, a company de Zavalía co-ran with Luis Saslavsky. It marked de Zavalía's feature film debut, and SIFAL disbanded the following year after Saslavsky completed his renowned film "Crimen a las tres."
