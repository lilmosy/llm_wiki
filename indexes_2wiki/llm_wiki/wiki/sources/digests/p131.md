# The Ballad of Josie (digest)

"The Ballad of Josie" (1967) is a Technicolor American comedy western directed by Andrew V. McLaglen, starring Doris Day, Peter Graves, and George Kennedy, which humorously explores 1960s feminist themes within a traditional Western setting. Filmed in Thousand Oaks, California, it also marked the final acting role of William Talman.
