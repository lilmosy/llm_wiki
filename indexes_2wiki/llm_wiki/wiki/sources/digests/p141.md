# Catherine of Pomerania, Countess Palatine of Neumarkt (digest)

Catherine of Pomerania (c. 1390–1426) was a Pomeranian princess who became Countess Palatine of Neumarkt through her marriage to John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt. She was the mother of Christopher of Bavaria, who later ruled Denmark, Sweden, and Norway as king of the Kalmar Union.
