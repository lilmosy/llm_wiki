# Les Trottoirs de Bangkok (digest)

"Les Trottoirs de Bangkok" (1984) is an erotic thriller directed by Jean Rollin, inspired by "The Mask of Fu Manchu" (1932). Departing from Rollin's typical vampire and gothic château themes, the film instead blends adventure, crime, and mystery with comic-book style dialogue, while retaining the nudity and sexual content characteristic of his work.
