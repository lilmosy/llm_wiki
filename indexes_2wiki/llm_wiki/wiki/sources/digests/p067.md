# Marie Charlotte de La Trémoille (digest)

Marie Charlotte de La Trémoille (1632–1682) was a French noblewoman of the House of La Trémoille, born in Thouars as the fifth of six children of Henri III de La Trémoille, 3rd Duke of Thouars, and Marie de La Tour d'Auvergne, and she became Duchess of Saxe-Jena through marriage.
