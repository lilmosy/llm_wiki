# The Blood of Fu Manchu (digest)

The Blood of Fu Manchu (1968) is a British adventure crime film directed by Jesús Franco, the fourth installment in the Fu Manchu series produced by Harry Alan Towers, starring Christopher Lee, Richard Greene, and Howard Marion-Crawford, and filmed in Spain and Brazil. Notably, Shirley Eaton was included via uncredited/unauthorized stock footage from her earlier film "The Girl from Rio," a fact she only discovered years later.
