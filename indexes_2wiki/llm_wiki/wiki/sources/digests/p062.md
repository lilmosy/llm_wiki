# Charlotte de La Trémoille (digest)

Charlotte Marie Clotilde de La Trémoille (1892–1971) was a French noblewoman who held numerous hereditary titles, including 12th Duchess of Thouars and 11th Duchess of La Trémoïlle, as the daughter of Prince Louis Charles Marie de La Trémoille and Hélène Marie Léonie Pillet-Will. She should not be confused with the earlier historical figure Charlotte Stanley, Countess of Derby.
