# Countess Palatine Magdalene Catherine of Zweibrücken (digest)

Magdalena Catherine, Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken (26 April 1607 – 20 January 1648), was a member of the House of Wittelsbach by birth who became Duchess and Countess Palatine of Birkenfeld through her marriage. She was born and died in the Palatinate region, with her death occurring in Strasbourg.
