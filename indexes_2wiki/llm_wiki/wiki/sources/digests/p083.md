# Rafaela Aparicio (digest)

Rafaela Aparicio (1906–1996) was a prolific Spanish film and theatre actress who appeared in over 100 films, most notably Carlos Saura's "Anna and the Wolves" and "Mama Turns 100," and Fernando Fernán Gómez's "El extraño viaje." She died of a stroke in a Madrid retirement home in 1996.
