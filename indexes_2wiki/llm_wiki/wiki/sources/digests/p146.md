# Countess Palatine Maria Franziska of Sulzbach (digest)

Countess Palatine Maria Franziska of Sulzbach (1724–1794) was the fifth child of Joseph Karl, Count Palatine of Sulzbach, and became Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld through her marriage to Frederick Michael. Of her siblings, only she and her elder sisters Elisabeth Auguste and Maria Anna survived to adulthood.
