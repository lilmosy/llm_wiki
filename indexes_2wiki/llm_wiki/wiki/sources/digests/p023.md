# The Mask of Fu Manchu (digest)

**The Mask of Fu Manchu** (1932) is a pre-Code adventure film directed by Charles Brabin, based on Sax Rohmer's novel of the same name, starring Boris Karloff as the titular villain and Myrna Loy as his daughter, with Lewis Stone as his rival. The plot centers on Fu Manchu's pursuit of Genghis Khan's golden sword and mask.
