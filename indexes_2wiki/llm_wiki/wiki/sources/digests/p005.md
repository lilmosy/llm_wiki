# Herschell Gordon Lewis (digest)

Herschell Gordon Lewis (1926–2016) was an American filmmaker known as the "Godfather of Gore" for pioneering the "splatter" horror subgenre, though his career also spanned various exploitation genres including juvenile delinquent films, nudie-cuties, and children's films. All Movie praised him as a pioneer who pushed boundaries of onscreen disgust with unmatched bad taste and imagination for his era.
