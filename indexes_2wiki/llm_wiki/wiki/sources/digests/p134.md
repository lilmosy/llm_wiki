# The Ballad of Little Joe (digest)

**Provenance summary:** "The Ballad of Little Joe" is a 2003 computer-animated western film and the 20th VeggieTales episode, produced by Big Idea Productions and released theatrically by Classic Media on August 5, 2003. Directed by Tim Hodge and written by Robert G. Lee, the film was inspired by the 1993 film "The Ballad of Little Jo" and features voice performances by Phil Vischer, Mike Nawrocki, and others, marking Mike Sage's final acting role.
