# Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille (digest)

Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille (1764–1839) was a French aristocrat and soldier who held numerous hereditary titles, including 8th Duke of Thouars and 7th Duke of La Trémoïlle. He was the son of Jean Bretagne Charles de La Trémoille and Princess Marie-Maximilienne of Salm-Kyrburg.
