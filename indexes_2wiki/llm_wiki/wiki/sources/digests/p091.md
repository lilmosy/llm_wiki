# Antiochus X Eusebes (digest)

Antiochus X Eusebes Philopator was a Seleucid king who ruled Syria (c. 95–92/88 BC) amid civil wars and foreign interventions, killing his father's rival Seleucus VI before facing further conflicts with Seleucus's brothers; he married his stepmother Cleopatra Selene, and his death remains disputed among ancient sources, with Josephus's account of him dying fighting the Parthians considered most reliable by modern scholars.
