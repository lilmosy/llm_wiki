# Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (digest)

Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (died 17 July 1362), was a member of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst; he was the third son of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, and the eldest child from Albert I's second marriage to Agnes, daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal.
