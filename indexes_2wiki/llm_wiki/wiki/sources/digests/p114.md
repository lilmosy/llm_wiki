# John Sturges (digest)

John Sturges (1910–1992) was an American film director known for classics such as "Bad Day at Black Rock," "Gunfight at the O.K. Corral," "The Magnificent Seven," "The Great Escape," and "Ice Station Zebra." His 1960 film "The Magnificent Seven" was added to the National Film Registry in 2013 for its cultural, historical, or aesthetic significance.
