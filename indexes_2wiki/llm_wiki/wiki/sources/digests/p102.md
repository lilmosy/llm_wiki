# Luis Saslavsky (digest)

Luis Saslavsky (1903–1995) was a prominent Jewish Argentine film director, screenwriter, and producer who worked in Argentine cinema's classic era, directing and writing over 40 films between 1931 and 1979. Born in Rosario and later based in Buenos Aires, where he died, his notable works include "Crimen a las tres" (1935) and "Allá en el Norte" (1973).
