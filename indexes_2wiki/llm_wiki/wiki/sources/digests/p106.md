# The Mattei Affair (digest)

"The Mattei Affair" (1972), directed by Francesco Rosi and starring Gian Maria Volonté, is a documentary-fiction hybrid film depicting the life and unsolved death of Italian oil executive Enrico Mattei, notable for winning the Grand Prix at Cannes and for incorporating footage related to the still-unsolved disappearance of journalist Mauro De Mauro, who was investigating the case for the film.
