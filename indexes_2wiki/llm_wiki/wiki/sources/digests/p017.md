# Snow White and the Three Stooges (digest)

Released by 20th Century Fox in 1961, "Snow White and the Three Stooges" was the second feature film starring the Three Stooges (Moe Howard, Larry Fine, and Joe DeRita) following their career resurgence, and was Walter Lang's final directorial effort before retirement. The film, retitled "Snow White and the Three Clowns" in Great Britain, cast Olympic figure skater Carol Heiss as Snow White in this comedic adaptation of the classic fairy tale.
