# Xawery Żuławski (digest)

Xawery Żuławski (born 1971) is a Polish film director and 1995 graduate of the National Film School in Łódź, son of actress Małgorzata Braunek and director Andrzej Żuławski. His notable work includes "Wojna polsko-ruska" (2009), which won First Prize at the 9th Era New Horizons Film Festival.
