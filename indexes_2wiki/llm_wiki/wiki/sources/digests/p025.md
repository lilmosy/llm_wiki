# The Brides of Fu Manchu (digest)

The Brides of Fu Manchu (1966) is a British/West German adventure crime film, the second in Harry Alan Towers' Fu Manchu series based on Sax Rohmer's villain, directed by Don Sharp with Christopher Lee reprising the title role and Douglas Wilmer replacing Nigel Green as Nayland Smith. Set mainly in London, it followed The Face of Fu Manchu and preceded three further sequels released between 1967 and 1969.
