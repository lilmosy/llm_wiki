# Waldemar III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (digest)

Waldemar III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (d. 1391), was a German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst. He was the youngest son of John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, and his wife Elisabeth, daughter of John I, Count of Henneberg-Schleusingen.
