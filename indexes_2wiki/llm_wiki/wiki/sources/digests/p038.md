# Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau (digest)

Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau (d. 12 June 1516) was a German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Dessau. He was the second son of George I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau, and the first son born to George's fourth wife, Anna of Lindow-Ruppin.
