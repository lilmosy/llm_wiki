# Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (digest)

Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (d. 7 January 1368), was a German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst. He was the youngest son of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, and his second wife Agnes, daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal.
