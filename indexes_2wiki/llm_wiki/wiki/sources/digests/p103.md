# The Working Class Goes to Heaven (digest)

"The Working Class Goes to Heaven" (1971), directed by Elio Petri and released in the US as "Lulu the Tool," is an Italian political drama depicting a factory worker's growing awareness of his role as a mere "tool" in production and his conflict with trade unions. The film tied for the Grand Prix International du Festival at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival and won the David di Donatello for Best Film in Italy.
