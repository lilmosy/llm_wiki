# Henri Charles de La Trémoille (digest)

Henri Charles de La Trémoille (1620–1672) was a French military commander and son of Henry de La Trémoille, duc of Thouars, whose life was marked by shifting religious allegiances between Catholicism and Protestantism and political opposition to Cardinal Mazarin during the Fronde, leading to his imprisonment. He predeceased his father, and his son Charles succeeded as the fourth duke of Thouars.
