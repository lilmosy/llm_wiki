# Charles Armand René de La Trémoille (digest)

Charles Armand René de La Trémoille (1708–1741), 6th duc de Thouars, was a French soldier, president of the States of Brittany, and member of the Académie française (elected 1738), known for military service including distinction at the Battle of Guastalla. He married his cousin Marie Hortense de La Tour d'Auvergne in 1725, with whom he had a son, Jean Bretagne Charles de La Trémoille, and a daughter who died young.
