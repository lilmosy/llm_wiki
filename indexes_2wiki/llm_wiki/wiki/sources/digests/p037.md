# John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (digest)

John V of Anhalt-Zerbst (1504–1551) was a German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Dessau and, from 1544, the re-created principality of Anhalt-Zerbst. He was the second but eldest surviving son of Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau, and Margarete of Münsterberg-Oels, granddaughter of King George of Poděbrady of Bohemia.
