# The Face of Fu Manchu (digest)

**Summary:** *The Face of Fu Manchu* (1965) is a British-West German co-production directed by Don Sharp, starring Christopher Lee as the villainous Fu Manchu and Nigel Green as his rival Nayland Smith. It was the first of five films in the series produced by Harry Alan Towers, shot in Technicolor and Techniscope on location in County Dublin, Ireland.
