# Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (digest)

Bernhard VII of Anhalt-Zerbst (1540–1570) was a German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst. Born and died in Dessau, he was the youngest of three sons of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, and Margaret of Brandenburg.
