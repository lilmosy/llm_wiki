# Ottawa High School and Junior High School (digest)

The Ottawa High School and Junior High School in Ottawa, Kansas, were built in 1917 and 1927–1928 respectively by the architecture firm George P. Washburn & Son (later Washburn & Stookey) in the Collegiate Gothic style. After serving various educational functions through the 20th century, including as a middle school, the connected buildings were listed on the National Register of Historic Places on October 6, 2000.
