# Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella (digest)

Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella is a 1970 Burmese film directed by Maung Wunna, marking his directorial debut, and starring Myat Mon, Myat Lay, and Thet Naung. Wunna also wrote the screenplay, adapting it from Maung Thara's novel of the same name.
