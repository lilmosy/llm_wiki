# John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt (digest)

John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt (1383–1443), was the son of Rupert III of the Palatinate who ruled Neumarkt from 1410 until his death. He married Catherine of Pomerania in 1407 and is best remembered for his decisive victory over the Hussites at the Battle of Hiltersried in 1433.
