# Countess Palatine Anna Magdalena of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler (digest)

Countess Palatine Anna Magdalena of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler (1640–1693) was born in Strasbourg and died in Babenhausen. She was the daughter of Christian I, Count Palatine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler, and his first wife, Countess Palatine Magdalene Catherine of Zweibrücken.
