# John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (digest)

John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst (d. 11 April 1382), a member of the House of Ascania, ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst as the youngest son of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, and his second wife Beatrix, daughter of Rudolf I, Elector of Saxony and Duke of Saxe-Wittenberg.
