# Countess Palatine Dorothea Catherine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler (digest)

Countess Palatine Dorothea Catherine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler (3 July 1634 – 7 December 1715) was born into the House of Palatinate-Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld-Bischweiler and became Countess of Nassau-Ottweiler through marriage.
