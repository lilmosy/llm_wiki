# Louis Charles de La Trémoille (digest)

Louis Charles de La Trémoille (1838–1911) was a French aristocrat holding numerous hereditary titles, including 9th Duke of Thouars and 8th Duke of La Trémoïlle, born to Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille and Valentine Eugénie Joséphine Walsh de Serrant. He was also known for his pursuits as an archivist, bibliophile, and collector.
