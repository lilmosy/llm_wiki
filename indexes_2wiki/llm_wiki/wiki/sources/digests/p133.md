# Eskimo Nell (film) (digest)

"Eskimo Nell" is a 1975 British film directed by Martin Campbell and produced by Stanley Long, also known by alternate titles including "The Ballad of Eskimo Nell" and "The Sexy Saga of Naughty Nell and Big Dick." While inspired by the bawdy song "The Ballad of Eskimo Nell," the film bears little resemblance to its source material.
