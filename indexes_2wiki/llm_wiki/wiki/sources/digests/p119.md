# Fleetwood Sheppard (digest)

Fleetwood Sheppard (1634–1698) was an English courtier, wit, and literary patron who served as a close companion to figures like Charles Sackville (Earl of Dorset) and Nell Gwynn during Charles II's court, later regaining favor under William III as a gentleman usher and knight of the garter. He was notable for discovering and supporting poets such as Matthew Prior and for his association with the era's literary and satirical circles.
