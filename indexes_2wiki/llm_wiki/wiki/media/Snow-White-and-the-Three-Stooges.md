# Snow White and the Three Stooges
> A 1961 20th Century Fox film adapting 'Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs' as a vehicle for the Three Stooges, and the trio's second feature after their resurgence in popularity.

## Key Facts
- The second feature film to star the Three Stooges after their 1959 resurgence in popularity
- Released by 20th Century Fox
- An adaptation of the classic fairy tale 'Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs'
- Retitled 'Snow White and the Three Clowns' in Great Britain
- Was director Walter Lang's final directing film before his retirement
- Starred Olympic gold medalist figure skater Carol Heiss as Snow White
- Plot: Snow White flees home after the Evil Queen, her evil stepmother, wishes her dead, then meets the Three Stooges who are house-sitting for the seven dwarfs
