# Les Trottoirs de Bangkok
> 1984 erotic thriller film directed by Jean Rollin, inspired by The Mask of Fu Manchu.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1984
- Genre: erotic thriller
- Directed by Jean Rollin
- Also known as 'The Sidewalks of Bangkok' (English) or 'Bangkok Interdit'
- Inspired by the 1932 Boris Karloff film 'The Mask of Fu Manchu'
- Departs from Rollin's usual themes of vampires, dream-like atmosphere, and crumbling châteaus
- Mixes themes of adventure, crime and mystery with comic book dialogue
- Still features nudity and sex scenes typical of Rollin's films
