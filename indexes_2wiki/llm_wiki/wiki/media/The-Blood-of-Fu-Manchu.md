# The Blood of Fu Manchu
> 1968 British adventure crime film directed by Jesús Franco, the fourth in the Fu Manchu film series based on Sax Rohmer's villain.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1968 as a British adventure crime film
- Also known by alternate titles: Fu Manchu and the Kiss of Death, Kiss of Death, Kiss and Kill (U.S. title), and Against All Odds (original U.S. video title)
- Directed by Jesús Franco
- Based on the fictional Asian villain Dr. Fu Manchu created by Sax Rohmer
- Fourth film in the Fu Manchu film series, preceded by The Vengeance of Fu Manchu, followed by The Castle of Fu Manchu in 1969
- Produced by Harry Alan Towers for Udastex Films
- Starred Christopher Lee as Dr. Fu Manchu, Richard Greene as Scotland Yard detective Nayland Smith, and Howard Marion-Crawford as Dr. Petrie
- Filmed in Spain and Brazil
- Contains stock footage of Shirley Eaton inserted from her film The Girl from Rio (1968) without her knowledge or payment
