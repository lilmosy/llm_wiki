# The Girl in Number 29
> 1920 American silent drama film directed by John Ford, based on a 1919 novel by Elizabeth Jordan; now presumed lost.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1920 in the United States
- Silent drama film
- Directed by John Ford
- Based on the novel "The Girl in the Mirror" (1919) by Elizabeth Jordan
- The film is presumed to be lost
