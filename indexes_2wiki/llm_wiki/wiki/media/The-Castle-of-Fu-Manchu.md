# The Castle of Fu Manchu
> 1969 film that is the fifth and final entry in the Dr. Fu Manchu film series starring Christopher Lee.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1969
- Fifth and final Dr. Fu Manchu film
- Also known as The Torture Chamber of Dr. Fu Manchu
- German title: Die Folterkammer des Dr. Fu Man Chu
- Christopher Lee portrayed the title character, Dr. Fu Manchu, for the fifth and final time
