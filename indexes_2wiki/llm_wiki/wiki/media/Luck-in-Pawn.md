# Luck in Pawn
> 1919 American silent romance film starring Marguerite Clark, based on a play of the same name.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1919 as an American silent romance film
- Produced by Famous Players-Lasky
- Distributed through Paramount Pictures
- Based on a play by Marvin Taylor titled 'Luck in Pawn' that ran briefly on Broadway in 1919
- Preserved at the Library of Congress and the New Zealand Film Archive
