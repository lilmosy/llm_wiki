# A Snow White Christmas
> A 1980 Christmas animated television special produced by Filmation, serving as a sequel to the fairy tale Snow White.

## Key Facts
- Telecast on December 19, 1980, on CBS
- Produced by Filmation
- Is a sequel to the fairy tale 'Snow White'
- Unrelated to Filmation's other 'Snow White' sequel, 'Happily Ever After' (1990)
- Plot centers on the Wicked Queen's return to life during Christmas, casting a spell that freezes the land
- The young Snow White, daughter of the original Snow White, escapes with her dwarf friend and takes refuge with the seven giants
- The giants must defeat the Queen to save the kingdom
