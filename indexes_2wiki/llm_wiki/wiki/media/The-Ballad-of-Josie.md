# The Ballad of Josie
> A 1967 Technicolor American comedy western film blending 1960s feminist themes with a traditional Western setting.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1967
- Directed by Andrew V. McLaglen
- Starred Doris Day, Peter Graves and George Kennedy
- Humorously tackles 1960s themes of feminism in a traditional Western setting
- Featured the last acting role for William Talman
- Filmed on two locations in Thousand Oaks, California: North Ranch and Wildwood Regional Park
