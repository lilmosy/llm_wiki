# The Magnificent Seven
> 1960 American Western film directed by John Sturges, later selected for preservation in the U.S. National Film Registry.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1960
- Directed by John Sturges
- Selected in 2013 for preservation in the United States National Film Registry by the Library of Congress
- Deemed 'culturally, historically, or aesthetically significant' by the Library of Congress
