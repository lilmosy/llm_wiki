# What is God?
> 1989 children's book by Etan Boritzer that gained national critical acclaim and sparked controversy for its universalist views on God.

## Key Facts
- Written by Etan Boritzer
- First published in 1989
- Boritzer's best-known book
- Gained national critical acclaim after publication
- Caused controversy from religious fundamentalists for its universalist views
- Part of the 'What is?' illustrated children's book series
