# Invasion from Inner Earth
> 1974 apocalyptic science fiction film starring Paul Bentzen and Debbi Pick, directed by Bill Rebane.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1974
- Genre: apocalyptic science fiction
- Also known as Hell Fire and They
- Inspired by the British film The Strange World of Planet X (1957)
- Filmed in the style of thrillers directed by Alfred Hitchcock
