# 1632 (novel)
> A popular alternative history novel by Eric Flint set in the fictional town of Grantville, based on Mannington, West Virginia and its surroundings.

## Key Facts
- Written by Eric Flint
- Popular alternative history novel
- Set in the fictional town of Grantville
- Grantville is based on the real town and surroundings of Mannington, West Virginia
- North Marion High School is represented in the novel as "Grantville High School"
