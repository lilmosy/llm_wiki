# Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella
> 1970 Burmese film directed by Maung Wunna, based on a novel by Maung Thara.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1970
- Burmese-language film
- Directed by Maung Wunna, marking his directorial debut
- Stars Myat Mon, Myat Lay and Thet Naung
- Screenplay written by Maung Wunna
- Based on the novel of the same name by Maung Thara
- Title pronounced as 'Katipa phanat see shwe htee hsaung'
