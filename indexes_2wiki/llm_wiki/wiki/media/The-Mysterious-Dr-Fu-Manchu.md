# The Mysterious Dr. Fu Manchu
> 1929 American pre-Code film that is the first entry in the talkie-era Fu Manchu film series, preceding The Return of Dr. Fu Manchu.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1929 in the United States
- A pre-Code American drama film
- First Fu Manchu film of the talkie era
- A silent version was also released in the United States due to the transition period to sound
- Depicts the apparent death of Dr. Fu Manchu, which is referenced as backstory in the sequel The Return of Dr. Fu Manchu (1930)
