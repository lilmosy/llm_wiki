# What is? series
> Best-selling illustrated children's book series by Etan Boritzer on character education and difficult subjects for children, translated into 15 languages.

## Key Facts
- Created by Etan Boritzer
- Best-selling illustrated children's book series on character education and difficult subjects for children
- Popular teaching guide for parents, teachers, and child-life professionals
- Includes 'What is God?' (first published 1989) and other titles: What is Love?, What is Death?, What is Beautiful?, What is Funny?, What is Right?, What is Peace?, What is Money?, What is Dreaming?, What is a Friend?, What is True?, What is a Family?, What is a Feeling?
- Now translated into 15 languages
