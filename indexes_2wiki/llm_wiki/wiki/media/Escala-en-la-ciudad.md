# Escala en la ciudad
> Argentine film directed and written by Alberto De Zavalia with Carlos Aden, produced by SIFAL.

## Key Facts
- Argentine film whose title translates to 'A stop in the city'
- Directed and written by Alberto De Zavalia with Carlos Aden
- Produced by the production company SIFAL
- This was Alberto De Zavalia's feature film debut
