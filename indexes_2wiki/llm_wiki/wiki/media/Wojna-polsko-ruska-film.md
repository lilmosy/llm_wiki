# Wojna polsko-ruska (film)
> 2009 Polish film directed by Xawery Żuławski, adapted from Dorota Masłowska's controversial best-selling novel.

## Key Facts
- Released in 2009
- Xawery Żuławski's second feature film
- Adapted from the controversial best-selling novel by Dorota Masłowska
- Won First Prize in the New Polish Films competition at the 9th Era New Horizons Film Festival in Wrocław
