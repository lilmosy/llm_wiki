# The Return of Dr. Fu Manchu
> 1930 American pre-Code film directed by Rowland V. Lee, the second of three films starring Warner Oland as Dr. Fu Manchu.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1930 in the United States
- A pre-Code American film
- Directed by Rowland V. Lee
- Second of three films starring Warner Oland as Dr. Fu Manchu
- Plot: Fu Manchu returns from apparent death depicted in the previous film, The Mysterious Dr. Fu Manchu (1929), to seek revenge on those he holds responsible for the death of his wife and child
