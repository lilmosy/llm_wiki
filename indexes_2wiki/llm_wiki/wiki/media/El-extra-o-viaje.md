# El extraño viaje
> 1964 Spanish black drama film directed by Fernando Fernán Gómez, later acclaimed as a classic of Spanish cinema.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1964 in Spain
- Genre is black drama/comedy
- Directed by Fernando Fernán Gómez
- Jess Franco appears in the film as the brother of the protagonist
- Was a huge flop on its limited theatrical release
- Voted seventh best Spanish film by professionals and critics during the 1996 Spanish cinema centenary
