# The Girl in the Mirror
> 1919 novel by Elizabeth Jordan that served as the basis for the 1920 film The Girl in Number 29.

## Key Facts
- Published in 1919
- Written by Elizabeth Jordan
- Adapted into the 1920 film The Girl in Number 29, directed by John Ford
