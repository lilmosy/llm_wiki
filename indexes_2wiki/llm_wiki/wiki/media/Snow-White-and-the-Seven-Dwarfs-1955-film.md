# Snow-White-and-the-Seven-Dwarfs-1955-film
> A 1955 German film adaptation of the Brothers Grimm's Schneewittchen, released in the USA as 'Snow White'.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1955 as a German film
- Released under the title 'Snow White' in the USA
- Directed by Erich Kobler
- Based on the story of Schneewittchen by the Brothers Grimm
