# The Girl in the Taxi (1937 film)
> 1937 British musical comedy film directed by André Berthomieu, based on the stage musical of the same name.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1937
- A British musical comedy film
- Directed by André Berthomieu
- Starred Frances Day, Henri Garat and Lawrence Grossmith
- Based on the stage musical 'The Girl in the Taxi'
