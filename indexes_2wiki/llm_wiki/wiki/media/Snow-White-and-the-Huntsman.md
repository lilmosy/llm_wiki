# Snow White and the Huntsman
> A 2012 fantasy film directed by Rupert Sanders, featuring Liberty Ross as Queen Eleanor.

## Key Facts
- A film for which Pakistani designer Maheen Khan undertook embroidery work.
- Released in 2012 as a fantasy film.
- Directed by Rupert Sanders.
- Starred Liberty Ross in the role of Queen Eleanor.
- Featured embroidery work by Pakistani designer Maheen Khan.
