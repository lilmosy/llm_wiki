# Blind Shaft
> 2003 film about con artists exploiting illegal coal mines in northern China, directed by Li Yang.

## Key Facts
- Released in 2003
- Depicts a pair of brutal con artists operating in the illegal coal mines of present-day northern China
- Based on the short novel 'Shen Mu' (Sacred Wood) by Liu Qingbang
