# The Working Class Goes to Heaven
> 1971 Italian political drama film directed by Elio Petri, released in the US as Lulu the Tool.

## Key Facts
- Released in the US under the title Lulu the Tool
- A 1971 political drama film directed by Elio Petri
- Depicts a factory worker's realisation of his own condition as a simple 'tool' in the process of production
- Implicitly explores the worker's struggle with the trade unions
- Competed at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival, tying with The Mattei Affair for the Grand Prix International du Festival
- Won the David di Donatello for Best Film in Italy
- Shared the Grand Prix with 'The Mattei Affair' at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival
- Starred Gian Maria Volonté as the leading actor
- Competed at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival, tying with The Mattei Affair for the Grand Prix International du Festival, the festival's highest honour
