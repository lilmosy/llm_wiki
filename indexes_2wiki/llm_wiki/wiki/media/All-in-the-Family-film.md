> 1975 Hong Kong adult comedy film directed by Mu Zhu and produced by Golden Harvest

## Key Facts

- Genre is adult comedy
- Produced under Golden Harvest productions
- Jackie Chan stars in the film but does not appear until 1 hour into the movie
