# The Capture of Bigfoot
> 1979 horror film directed by Bill Rebane, originally released by Studio Film Corp and later re-released by Troma Entertainment.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1979
- Also known as 'The Legend of Bigfoot'
- Directed by Bill Rebane
- Originally produced and released by Studio Film Corp
- Re-released in 2010 by Troma Entertainment
