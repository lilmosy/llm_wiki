# Monster A Go-Go
> 1965 American science-fiction horror film co-directed by Bill Rebane and an uncredited Herschell Gordon Lewis, often cited as one of the worst films ever made.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1965
- Directed by Bill Rebane
- Co-directed by Herschell Gordon Lewis, who remained uncredited
- Widely considered one of the worst films ever made
- Featured in a 1993 episode of the TV series 'Mystery Science Theater 3000' on Comedy Central
