# Eskimo Nell (film)
> 1975 British film directed by Martin Campbell and produced by Stanley Long, loosely inspired by the bawdy song of the same name.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1975 in the United Kingdom.
- Also known as 'The Ballad of Eskimo Nell' and 'The Sexy Saga of Naughty Nell and Big Dick'.
- Inspired by the traditional bawdy song 'The Ballad of Eskimo Nell', though it owes little to the original.
