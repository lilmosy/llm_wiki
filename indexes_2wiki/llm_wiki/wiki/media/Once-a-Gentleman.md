# Once a Gentleman
> 1930 American comedy film about a butler mistaken for a wealthy man while on vacation.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1930
- Genre: comedy film
- Directed by James Cruze
- Starred Edward Everett Horton, Lois Wilson and Francis X. Bushman
- Plot: A butler goes on vacation, where he is wrongly supposed to be a wealthy man.
