# The Mattei Affair
> 1972 Italian film that tied with The Working Class Goes to Heaven for the Grand Prix at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival.

## Key Facts
- Tied with The Working Class Goes to Heaven for the Grand Prix International du Festival at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival
- Directed by Francesco Rosi and released in 1972
- Depicts the life and mysterious death of Enrico Mattei, who developed Italy's oil and hydrocarbon industry via Eni
- Shared the Grand Prix with 'The Working Class Goes to Heaven' at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival
- Starred Gian Maria Volonté as the leading actor
- An innovative hybrid of documentary and fiction, exemplifying Rosi's concept of cine-inchieste (film investigation)
- Its flashback structure shows the influence of 'Citizen Kane' and Rosi's own 'Salvatore Giuliano' (1962)
- Uses on-location shooting and non-professional actors, staying faithful to neo-realist filmmaking
- Interspersed with footage of Rosi searching for journalist Mauro De Mauro, who disappeared while researching the film
- De Mauro was killed by the Sicilian Mafia, and like Mattei's death, his case was never solved
