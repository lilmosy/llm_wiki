# The Mask of Fu Manchu
> A 1932 pre-Code adventure film about Fu Manchu's quest for the golden sword and mask of Genghis Khan.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1932
- Stars Boris Karloff
- Inspired the 1984 film Les Trottoirs de Bangkok
- Released in 1932 as a pre-Code adventure film
- Directed by Charles Brabin
- Written by Irene Kuhn, Edgar Allan Woolf and John Willard, based on the 1932 novel of the same name by Sax Rohmer
- Stars Boris Karloff as Fu Manchu
- Features Myrna Loy as Fu Manchu's depraved daughter
- Lewis Stone plays Fu Manchu's nemesis
- The plot revolves around Fu Manchu's quest for the golden sword and mask of Genghis Khan
- The character Dr. Petrie is absent from this film
