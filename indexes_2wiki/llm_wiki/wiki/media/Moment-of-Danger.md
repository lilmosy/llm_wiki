# Moment of Danger
> 1960 crime drama film starring Trevor Howard, Dorothy Dandridge and Edmund Purdom, also known as Malaga.

## Key Facts
- Released in 1960 as a crime drama film
- Also known by the alternate title Malaga
- Filmed in Europe in the late months of 1959
- Based on a novel by Donald Mackenzie
- Brought to the screen by producer David D. Osborn
- This was the final completed film for actress Dorothy Dandridge
