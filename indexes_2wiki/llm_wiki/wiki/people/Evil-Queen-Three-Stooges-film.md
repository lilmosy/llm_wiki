# Evil Queen (Three Stooges film)
> The evil stepmother of Snow White in 'Snow White and the Three Stooges' who wishes her dead, prompting Snow White to flee.

## Key Facts
- Is Snow White's evil stepmother in 'Snow White and the Three Stooges'
- Wishes Snow White to be dead, causing Snow White to flee her home
