# Catherine of Pomerania, Countess Palatine of Neumarkt
> Pomeranian princess and Countess Palatine of Neumarkt, wife of John of Neumarkt and mother of Christopher of Bavaria

## Key Facts
- Born c. 1390, died 4 March 1426
- Was a Pomeranian princess and Countess Palatine of Neumarkt
- Wife of John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt
- Mother of Christopher of Bavaria, who ruled Denmark, Sweden, and Norway as king of the Kalmar Union
- Married John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt, in 1407
- German name: Katharina von Pommern
- Wife of John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt, whom she married in 1407
