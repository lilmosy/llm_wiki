# John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled Anhalt-Dessau and later Anhalt-Zerbst from 1544

## Key Facts
- Was the father of Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Married Margaret, daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
- Ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst before Bernhard VII
- Father of Karl I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Born on 4 September 1504 in Dessau; died 4 February 1551 in Zerbst
- Ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Dessau
- From 1544, assumed rule of the re-created principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Second but eldest surviving son of Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau
- Mother was Margarete, daughter of Henry I, Duke of Münsterberg-Oels
