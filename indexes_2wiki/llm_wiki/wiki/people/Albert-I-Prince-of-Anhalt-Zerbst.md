# Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania, father of Waldemar I and Albert II, Princes of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Was the father of Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Married Agnes, daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal, as his second wife
- Was the father of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, his third son and eldest child by Agnes
