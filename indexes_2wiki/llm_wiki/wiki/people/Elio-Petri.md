# Elio Petri
> Italian director of the 1971 political drama film The Working Class Goes to Heaven, best known for the Academy Award-winning Investigation of a Citizen Above Suspicion.

## Key Facts
- Directed the 1971 political drama film The Working Class Goes to Heaven
- Born 29 January 1929
- Died 10 November 1982
- Italian filmmaker specializing in political films
- Best known for directing 'Investigation of a Citizen Above Suspicion' (1970)
- Best known for directing 'Investigation of a Citizen Above Suspicion' (1970), which won an Academy Award
