# Maria Josepha of Saxony
> Mother of Marie Zéphyrine of France, wife of Louis, Dauphin of France

## Key Facts
- Mother of Marie Zéphyrine of France
- Suffered stillbirths in the two years prior to Marie Zéphyrine's birth
- Her health was of a fragile nature around the time of Marie Zéphyrine's birth
