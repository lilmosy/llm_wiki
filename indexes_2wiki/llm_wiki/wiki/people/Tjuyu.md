# Tjuyu
> Egyptian noblewoman, wife of Yuya and mother of queen Tiye

## Key Facts
- Sometimes transliterated as Thuya or Thuyu
- Was an Egyptian noblewoman
- Mother of queen Tiye
- Wife of Yuya
- Grandmother of Akhenaten
- Great grandmother of Tutankhamun
