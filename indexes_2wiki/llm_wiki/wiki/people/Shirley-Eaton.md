# Shirley Eaton
> Actress whose unauthorized stock footage from The Girl from Rio (1968) was inserted into The Blood of Fu Manchu without her knowledge or payment.

## Key Facts
- Appears in a scene in The Blood of Fu Manchu that she claimed she was never paid for
- Director Jesús Franco inserted stock footage of her from her film The Girl from Rio (1968) into The Blood of Fu Manchu without telling her
- Only learned years later that she had appeared in a Fu Manchu film
