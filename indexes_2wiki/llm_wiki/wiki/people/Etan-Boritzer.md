# Etan Boritzer
> American writer of children's literature born in 1950, best known for 'What is God?' and the 'What is?' book series.

## Key Facts
- Born in 1950
- American writer of children's literature
- Best known for the book 'What is God?', first published in 1989
- Gained national critical acclaim after 'What is God?' was published in 1989
- The book caused controversy from religious fundamentalists for its universalist views
- First published in 1963 at age 13 when he wrote an essay in his English class at Wade Junior High School in the Bronx, New York, on the assassination of John F. Kennedy
- His essay was included in a special anthology by New York City public school children compiled and published by the New York City Department of Education
- Now lives in Venice, California and maintains his publishing office there
- Has helped numerous other authors get published through his 'How to Get Your Book Published!' programs
- Is also a yoga teacher who teaches regular classes locally and guest-teaches nationally
- Recognized nationally as an erudite speaker on 'The Teachings of the Buddha'
