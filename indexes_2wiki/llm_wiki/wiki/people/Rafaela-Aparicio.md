# Rafaela Aparicio
> Famous Spanish film and theatre actress known for roles in over 100 films.

## Key Facts
- Born Rafaela Díaz Valiente on 9 April 1906
- Died on 9 June 1996 of a stroke in a retirement home in Madrid
- Appeared in more than 100 films
- Best remembered for her roles in Carlos Saura's 'Ana and the Wolves' and its sequel 'Mama Turns 100', as well as Fernando Fernán Gómez's 'El extraño viaje'
- Made more than 100 films
- Best remembered for roles in Carlos Saura's 'Anna and the Wolves' and 'Mama Turns 100', and Fernando Fernán Gómez's 'El extraño viaje'
