# Louis, Dauphin of France (son of Louis XV)
> Heir apparent to the French throne who died before ascending, father of three future kings of France

## Key Facts
- Born 4 September 1729, died 20 December 1765
- Elder and only surviving son of King Louis XV of France and Queen Marie Leszczyńska
- Had a younger brother, Philippe, who died as a toddler
- As a son of the king, held the rank of 'fils de France'
- Became Dauphin of France as heir apparent
- Died before ascending to the throne
- Three of his sons became kings of France: Louis XVI, Louis XVIII, and Charles X
