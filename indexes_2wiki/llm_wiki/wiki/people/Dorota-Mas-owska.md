# Dorota Masłowska
> Author of the controversial best-selling novel adapted into the film 'Wojna polsko-ruska'.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the novel 'Polish-Russian War under the white-red flag'
- Her novel was adapted into the 2009 film 'Polish-Russian War'
- Wrote the controversial best-selling novel that was adapted into 'Wojna polsko-ruska' (2009)
