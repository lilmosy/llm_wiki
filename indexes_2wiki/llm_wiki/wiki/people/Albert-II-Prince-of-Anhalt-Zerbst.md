# Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania, father of John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, through his second wife Beatrix.

## Key Facts
- Died on 17 July 1362
- Was a German prince of the House of Ascania
- Ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the third son of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the eldest child born to Albert I's second wife, Agnes, daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal
- Married Beatrix, daughter of Rudolf I, Elector of Saxony and Duke of Saxe-Wittenberg, as his second wife
- Father of Albert III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was a Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst of the House of Ascania
- Had a second wife named Beatrix, daughter of Rudolf I, Elector of Saxony
- Was the father of John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, his youngest son by Beatrix
