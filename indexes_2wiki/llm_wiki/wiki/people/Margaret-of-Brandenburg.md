# Margaret of Brandenburg
> Daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg, who married John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Was the daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
- Was the wife of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the mother of Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
- Wife of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Mother of Karl I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
