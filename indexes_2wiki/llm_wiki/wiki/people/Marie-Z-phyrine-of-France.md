# Marie Zéphyrine of France
> French princess who died in childhood, daughter of Louis, Dauphin of France and Maria Josepha of Saxony

## Key Facts
- Born 26 August 1750 at the Palace of Versailles
- Died 2 September 1755 at Versailles due to an attack of convulsions
- Named after St Zephyrinus, on whose feast day she was born
- Known as 'Madame Royale' or 'la Petite Madame'
- Her birth was greeted with caution due to her mother's previous stillbirths and fragile health
- Louis XV had hoped for a grandson at her birth
- Baptised just days before her death by the Abbot of Chabannes
- Not officially mourned, as a French princess could only be mourned if over the age of 7
- Buried at the Royal Basilica of Saint Denis outside Paris
