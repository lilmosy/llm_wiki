# Maung Thara
> Burmese novelist who wrote the novel that inspired the 1970 film Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the novel Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella
- His novel was adapted into a 1970 film of the same name directed by Maung Wunna
- The novel was adapted into a 1970 film of the same name directed by Maung Wunna
