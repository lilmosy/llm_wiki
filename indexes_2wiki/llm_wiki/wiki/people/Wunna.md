# Wunna
> Two-time Myanmar Motion Picture Academy Awards-winning Burmese director and writer who died of lung cancer in 2011.

## Key Facts
- Full name Maung Wunna
- Won the Myanmar Motion Picture Academy Award twice
- Worked as a director and writer
- Came from a family heavily involved in the entertainment industry
- Died of lung cancer in 2011
- Was a two-time Myanmar Motion Picture Academy Awards-winning Burmese director and writer
- Directed the 1970 film Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella, his directorial debut
- Wrote the screenplay for Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella, based on a novel by Maung Thara
