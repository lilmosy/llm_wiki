# Charles Saunders (director)
> English film director and screenwriter who began his career as a film editor and later worked in television.

## Key Facts
- Full name Charles Joel Saunders
- Born 8 April 1904
- Died April 1997
- English film director and screenwriter
- Started in the industry as a film editor
- Also contributed to television
- Brother of theatrical producer Sir Peter Saunders
