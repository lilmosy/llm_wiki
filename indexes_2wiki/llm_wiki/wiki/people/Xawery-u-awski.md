# Xawery Żuławski
> Polish film director born 1971, son of actress Małgorzata Braunek and director Andrzej Żuławski.

## Key Facts
- Directed the 2009 film 'Polish-Russian War' (Wojna polsko-ruska)
- Born 22 December 1971 in Warsaw
- Graduated from the National Film School in Łódź in 1995
- Directed the film 'Wojna polsko-ruska' (2009), adapted from Dorota Masłowska's novel
- 'Wojna polsko-ruska' won First Prize in the New Polish Films competition at the 9th Era New Horizons Film Festival in Wrocław
- In 2013 stated he intends to direct a film adaptation of Leopold Tyrmand's novel 'Zły'
- Married to Maria Strzelecka
- Has two children with Maria Strzelecka: son Kaj Żuławski (born 2002) and daughter Jagna Żuławska (born 2009)
