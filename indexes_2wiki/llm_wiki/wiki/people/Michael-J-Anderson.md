# Michael J. Anderson
> Actor first met by David Lynch during casting for 'Ronnie Rocket', later cast in 'Twin Peaks' and 'Mulholland Drive'.

## Key Facts
- Met David Lynch during tentative casting for a three-foot tall character with control over electricity in 'Ronnie Rocket'
- Later cast by Lynch in 'Twin Peaks'
- Later cast by Lynch in 'Mulholland Drive'
