# David Lynch
> American filmmaker who wrote and intended to direct the unfinished project 'Ronnie Rocket'.

## Key Facts
- Wrote and intended to direct 'Ronnie Rocket'
- Directed the 1977 film 'Eraserhead', whose success led him to begin 'Ronnie Rocket'
- Shelved 'Ronnie Rocket' due to inability to secure financial backing
- Directed 'The Elephant Man' (1980) instead, based on an existing script
- Cast Michael J. Anderson in 'Twin Peaks' and 'Mulholland Drive' after meeting him during casting for 'Ronnie Rocket'
- Sought out an existing script after shelving 'Ronnie Rocket', settling on 'The Elephant Man' (1980)
- Met Michael J. Anderson while tentatively casting for a role in 'Ronnie Rocket'
- Later cast Michael J. Anderson in 'Twin Peaks' and 'Mulholland Drive'
