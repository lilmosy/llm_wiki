# Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
> Elector of Brandenburg and father of Margaret, who married into the House of Ascania.

## Key Facts
- Was Elector of Brandenburg
- Was the father of Margaret, wife of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Father of Margaret, who married John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Grandfather of Karl I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
