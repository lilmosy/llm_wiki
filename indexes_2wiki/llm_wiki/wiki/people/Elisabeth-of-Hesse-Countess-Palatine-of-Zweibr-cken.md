# Elisabeth of Hesse, Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken
> Landgravine of Hesse by birth who became Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken and later Countess Palatine of Simmern by marriage

## Key Facts
- Born 4 March 1503
- Died 4 January 1563 in Lauingen
- Landgravine of Hesse by birth
- Became Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken through marriage
- Later became Countess Palatine of Simmern through marriage
