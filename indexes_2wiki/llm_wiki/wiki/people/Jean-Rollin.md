# Jean Rollin
> French film director known for vampire and dream-like erotic horror films, who directed Les Trottoirs de Bangkok.

## Key Facts
- Directed Les Trottoirs de Bangkok (1984), an erotic thriller
- Usual thematic style involves vampires, dream-like atmosphere, and crumbling châteaus
- Known for including nudity and sex scenes in his films
