# Rudolf I, Elector of Saxony
> Elector of Saxony and Duke of Saxe-Wittenberg, father of Beatrix and maternal grandfather of John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Held the titles Elector of Saxony and Duke of Saxe-Wittenberg
- Father of Beatrix, who married Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the father of Beatrix, who married Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
