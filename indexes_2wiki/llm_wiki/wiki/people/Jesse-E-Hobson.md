# Jesse E. Hobson
> American administrator who directed SRI International and the Armour Research Foundation

## Key Facts
- Born May 2, 1911; died November 5, 1970
- Director of SRI International from 1947 to 1955
- Prior to SRI, he was director of the Armour Research Foundation
