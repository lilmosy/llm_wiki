# Christopher of Bavaria
> King of the Kalmar Union who ruled Denmark, Sweden, and Norway, son of Catherine of Pomerania and John of Neumarkt

## Key Facts
- Ruled over Denmark, Sweden, and Norway as king of the Kalmar Union
- Son of Catherine of Pomerania and John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt
- Son of Catherine of Pomerania, Countess Palatine of Neumarkt, and John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt
