# Countess Palatine Barbara of Zweibrücken-Neuburg
> Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken by birth who became Countess of Oettingen-Oettingen through marriage

## Key Facts
- Born 27 July 1559
- Died 5 March 1618
- Was a Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken by birth
- Became Countess of Oettingen-Oettingen by marriage
