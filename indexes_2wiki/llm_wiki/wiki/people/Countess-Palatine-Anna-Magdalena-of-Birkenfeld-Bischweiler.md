# Countess Palatine Anna Magdalena of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler
> German noblewoman of the Palatinate-Birkenfeld-Bischweiler branch of the House of Wittelsbach

## Key Facts
- Born on 14 February 1640 in Strasbourg
- Died on 12 December 1693 in Babenhausen
- Daughter of Christian I, Count Palatine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler and his first wife, Countess Palatine Magdalene Catherine of Zweibrücken
