# George William Whitaker
> Prominent Rhode Island landscape painter active in the late 19th and early 20th century.

## Key Facts
- Born September 25, 1840
- Died March 6, 1916
- Known as the 'Dean of Providence painters' or the 'Dean of Rhode Island Artists'
- Specialized in landscape painting
