# Margarete of Münsterberg-Oels
> Daughter of Henry I, Duke of Münsterberg-Oels, and wife of Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau

## Key Facts
- Daughter of Henry I, Duke of Münsterberg-Oels
- Granddaughter of George of Poděbrady, King of Bohemia
- Wife of Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau
- Mother of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
