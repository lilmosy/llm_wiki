# John VI, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst in the 17th century.

## Key Facts
- Born 24 March 1621 in Zerbst
- Died 4 July 1667 in Zerbst
- Was the only son of Rudolph, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- His mother was Magdalene, daughter of John VII, Count of Oldenburg, Rudolph's second wife
- Ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Member of the House of Ascania
