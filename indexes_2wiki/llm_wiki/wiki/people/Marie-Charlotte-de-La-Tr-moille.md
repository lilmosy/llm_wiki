# Marie Charlotte de La Trémoille
> French noblewoman of the House of La Trémoille who became Duchess of Saxe-Jena by marriage

## Key Facts
- Born 26 January 1632 in Thouars, France
- Died 24 August 1682
- Fifth of six children of Henri III de La Trémoille and Marie de La Tour d'Auvergne
- Became Duchess of Saxe-Jena through marriage
- Fifth of six children born from the marriage of Henri III de La Trémoille and Marie de La Tour d'Auvergne
- Member of the House of La Trémoille
