# S. N. Mathur
> Indian police officer who served as Director of the Intelligence Bureau and Director General of Police in Punjab

## Key Facts
- Served as Director of the Indian Intelligence Bureau between September 1975 and February 1980
- Also served as the Director General of Police in Punjab
