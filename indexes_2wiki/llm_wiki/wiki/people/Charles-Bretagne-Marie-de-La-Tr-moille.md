# Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille
> French aristocrat who held multiple hereditary titles and was the father of Louis Charles de La Trémoille.

## Key Facts
- Born 24 March 1764, died 10 November 1839
- Held the titles 8th Duke of Thouars, 7th Duke of La Trémoïlle, 10th prince de Tarente, 14th prince de Talmond, and 14th Count of Laval
- Was a French aristocrat and soldier
- Son of Jean Bretagne Charles de La Trémoille and Princess Marie-Maximilienne of Salm-Kyrburg
- Married three times; third wife was Valentine Eugénie Joséphine Walsh de Serrant
- Father of Louis Charles de La Trémoille
- Son of Jean Bretagne Charles de La Trémoille and his wife Princess Marie-Maximilienne of Salm-Kyrburg
