# Joe DeRita
> Member of the Three Stooges comedy trio, dubbed 'Curly Joe', who appeared in 'Snow White and the Three Stooges'.

## Key Facts
- Was dubbed 'Curly Joe' as a member of the Three Stooges
- Was one of the three members of the Three Stooges in 1961
- Appeared in 'Snow White and the Three Stooges'
