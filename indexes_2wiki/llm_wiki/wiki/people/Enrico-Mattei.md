# Enrico Mattei
> Italian businessman who built Eni and whose mysterious death is depicted in 'The Mattei Affair'.

## Key Facts
- Managed to avoid the sale of Italy's nascent oil and hydrocarbon industry to US companies after World War II
- Developed the industry into Eni, a state-owned oil company
- Eni rivaled the 'seven sisters' for oil and gas deals in North Africa and the Middle East
- His life and mysterious death are depicted in the 1972 film 'The Mattei Affair'
