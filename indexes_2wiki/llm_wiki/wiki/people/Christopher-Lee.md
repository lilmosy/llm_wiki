# Christopher-Lee
> Actor who played Dr. Fu Manchu in The Blood of Fu Manchu (1968).

## Key Facts
- Portrayed Dr. Fu Manchu, the title character, in The Castle of Fu Manchu (1969)
- This 1969 film was the fifth and final Dr. Fu Manchu film in which he played the role
- Starred as Dr. Fu Manchu in The Blood of Fu Manchu (1968)
