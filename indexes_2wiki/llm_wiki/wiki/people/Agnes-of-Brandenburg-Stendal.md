# Agnes of Brandenburg-Stendal
> Daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal, and second wife of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst; mother of Albert II.

## Key Facts
- Was the daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal
- Was the second wife of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the mother of Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the mother of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, her eldest child by Albert I
