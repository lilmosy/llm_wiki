# John, Count Palatine of Neumarkt
> Count Palatine of Neumarkt from 1410 until his death, husband of Catherine of Pomerania

## Key Facts
- Born in 1383 and died on 14 March 1443
- Count Palatine of Neumarkt from 1410 until his death
- Son of Rupert III of the Palatinate
- Married Catherine of Pomerania in 1407
- Led a crushing victory against the Hussites at the Battle of Hiltersried in 1433
- Married Catherine of Pomerania
- Father of Christopher of Bavaria, who ruled Denmark, Sweden, and Norway as king of the Kalmar Union
- Achieved a crushing victory against the Hussites at the Battle of Hiltersried in 1433
