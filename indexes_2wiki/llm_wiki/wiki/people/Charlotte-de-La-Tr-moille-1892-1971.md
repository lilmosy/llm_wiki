# Charlotte de La Trémoille (1892–1971)
> French noblewoman holding multiple hereditary titles, daughter of Prince Louis Charles Marie de La Trémoille and Hélène Marie Léonie Pillet-Will.

## Key Facts
- Born 20 November 1892, died 27 October 1971
- Held the titles 12th Duchess of Thouars, 11th Duchess of La Trémoïlle, 14th Princess de Tarente, 18th Princess de Talmond, and 18th Countess of Laval
- Not to be confused with Charlotte Stanley, Countess of Derby
- Was a French noblewoman
