# Anne Denman
> English woman born in Nottinghamshire whose second marriage linked her descendants to the English royal line

## Key Facts
- Born in 1587 and died in 1661
- Born in Olde Hall, Retford, Nottinghamshire
- Became grandmother of Lady Anne Hyde, Duchess of York through her second marriage to Thomas Aylesbury
- Great-grandmother of Queen Mary II and Queen Anne
