# Michael Govan
> American museum director born in 1963, currently leading the Los Angeles County Museum of Art.

## Key Facts
- Born in 1963
- Director of the Los Angeles County Museum of Art since 2006
- Previously worked as director of the Dia Art Foundation in New York City
- Previously served as director of the Dia Art Foundation in New York City
