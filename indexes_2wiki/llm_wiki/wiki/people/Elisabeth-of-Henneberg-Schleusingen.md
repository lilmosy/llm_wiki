# Elisabeth of Henneberg-Schleusingen
> Daughter of John I, Count of Henneberg-Schleusingen, and wife of John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst

## Key Facts
- Daughter of John I, Count of Henneberg-Schleusingen
- Married John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the mother of Waldemar III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
