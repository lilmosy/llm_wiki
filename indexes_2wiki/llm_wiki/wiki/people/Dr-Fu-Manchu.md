# Dr. Fu Manchu
> Recurring fictional villain who, in the 1930 film The Return of Dr. Fu Manchu, returns from apparent death to seek revenge.

## Key Facts
- Title character of The Mysterious Dr. Fu Manchu (1929), the first Fu Manchu film of the talkie era
- Portrayed by Warner Oland in this film
- Portrayed by Christopher Lee in The Castle of Fu Manchu (1969), the fifth and final film in the series with Lee in the role
- In The Return of Dr. Fu Manchu (1930), the character returns from apparent death suffered in the previous film
- Seeks revenge on those he holds responsible for the death of his wife and child
