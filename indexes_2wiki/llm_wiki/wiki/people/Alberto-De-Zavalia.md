# Alberto De Zavalia
> Argentine director and writer who made his feature film debut with Escala en la ciudad.

## Key Facts
- Directed and wrote the film Escala en la ciudad, with Carlos Aden
- Escala en la ciudad was his feature film debut
- Ran the production company SIFAL with his partner Luis Saslavsky
