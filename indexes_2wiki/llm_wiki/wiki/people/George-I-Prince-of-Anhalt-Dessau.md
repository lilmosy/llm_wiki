# George I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau
> German prince and ruler of Anhalt-Dessau, father of Ernest I through his fourth wife Anna.

## Key Facts
- Had multiple wives, with Anna, daughter of Albert VIII, Count of Lindow-Ruppin, being his fourth wife
- Fathered Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau, his second son, first born by his fourth wife
