# Jean Bretagne Charles de La Trémoille
> French prince and soldier who held the title of 7th Duke of Thouars

## Key Facts
- Married Princess Marie-Maximilienne of Salm-Kyrburg
- Father of Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille, 8th Duke of Thouars
- Born 5 February 1737, died 19 May 1792
- Held the title of 7th Duke of Thouars
- Served as a French soldier
- Son of Charles Armand René de La Trémoille and Marie Hortense de La Tour d'Auvergne of Bouillon
- Son of Charles Armand René de La Trémoille and his wife, Marie Hortense de La Tour d'Auvergne of Bouillon
