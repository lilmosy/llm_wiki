# Bill Rebane
> Film director known for Invasion from Inner Earth, Monster A Go-Go, The Giant Spider Invasion, and The Capture of Bigfoot.

## Key Facts
- Directed Invasion from Inner Earth (1974)
- Directed Monster A Go-Go (1965)
- Directed The Giant Spider Invasion (1975)
- Co-directed Monster A Go-Go (1965) with Herschell Gordon Lewis, who remained uncredited
- Directed The Capture of Bigfoot (1979), a.k.a. The Legend of Bigfoot
