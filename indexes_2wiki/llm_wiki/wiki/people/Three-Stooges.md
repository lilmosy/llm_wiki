# Three Stooges
> American comedy trio that experienced a resurgence in popularity starting in 1959, later starring in 'Snow White and the Three Stooges'.

## Key Facts
- Experienced a resurgence in popularity in 1959
- By the time of 'Snow White and the Three Stooges', consisted of Moe Howard, Larry Fine, and Joe DeRita (dubbed 'Curly Joe')
- Starred in 'Snow White and the Three Stooges', their second feature film after the 1959 resurgence
