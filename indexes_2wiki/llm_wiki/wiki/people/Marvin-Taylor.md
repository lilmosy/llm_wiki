# Marvin Taylor
> Playwright who wrote the play 'Luck in Pawn', which briefly ran on Broadway in 1919 and inspired the film of the same name.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the play 'Luck in Pawn'
- The play ran briefly on Broadway in 1919
- The play served as the basis for the 1919 film Luck in Pawn
