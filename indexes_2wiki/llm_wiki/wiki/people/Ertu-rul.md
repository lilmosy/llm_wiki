# Ertuğrul
> Figure in Ottoman tradition regarded as the grandson of Suleyman Shah and great grandfather of Osman I

## Key Facts
- According to Ottoman tradition, Ertuğrul was the grandson of Suleyman Shah
- According to Ottoman tradition, Ertuğrul was the great grandfather of Osman I, founder of the Ottoman Empire
