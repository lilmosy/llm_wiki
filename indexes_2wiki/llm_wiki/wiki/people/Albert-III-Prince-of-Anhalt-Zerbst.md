# Albert III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst until his death around 1359

## Key Facts
- Died circa 1 August 1359
- Ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Eldest son of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, by his second wife Beatrix
