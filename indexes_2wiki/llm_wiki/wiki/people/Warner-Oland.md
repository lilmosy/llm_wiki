# Warner Oland
> Actor who portrayed Dr. Fu Manchu in the first two films of the talkie-era Fu Manchu series.

## Key Facts
- Starred as Dr. Fu Manchu in The Mysterious Dr. Fu Manchu (1929)
- Starred as Dr. Fu Manchu in The Return of Dr. Fu Manchu (1930), the second of three films in which he played the role
