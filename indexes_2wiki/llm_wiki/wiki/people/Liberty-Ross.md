# Liberty Ross
> English model and actress known for magazine work and her role in Snow White and the Huntsman.

## Key Facts
- Born 23 September 1978.
- Has appeared in publications such as Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, i-D, and Dazed & Confused.
- Played Queen Eleanor in the 2012 fantasy film Snow White and the Huntsman.
- Was married to director Rupert Sanders, who directed Snow White and the Huntsman.
- Sister of composers Atticus Ross and Leopold Ross.
