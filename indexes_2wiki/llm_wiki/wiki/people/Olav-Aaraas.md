# Olav Aaraas
> Norwegian historian and museum director born in 1950, known for leading several major Norwegian museums.

## Key Facts
- Born 10 July 1950 in Fredrikstad, Norway
- Director of Sogn Folk Museum from 1982 to 1993
- Director of Maihaugen from 1993 to 2010
- Director of the Norwegian Museum of Cultural History since 2001
- Decorated with the Royal Norwegian Order of St. Olav in 2010
- Born on 10 July 1950
- Born in Fredrikstad, Norway
- Served as director of Sogn Folk Museum from 1982 to 1993
- Served as director of Maihaugen from 1993 to 2010
- Has served as director of the Norwegian Museum of Cultural History since 2001
