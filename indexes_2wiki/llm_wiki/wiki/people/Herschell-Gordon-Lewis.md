# Herschell-Gordon-Lewis
> American filmmaker credited with creating the 'splatter' subgenre of horror films, nicknamed the 'Godfather of Gore'.

## Key Facts
- Co-directed Monster A Go-Go (1965) alongside Bill Rebane
- Remained uncredited in association with Monster A Go-Go
- Born June 15, 1926; died September 26, 2016
- American filmmaker best known for creating the 'splatter' subgenre of horror films
- Often called the 'Godfather of Gore', a title also shared with Lucio Fulci
- Worked across a range of exploitation film genres including juvenile delinquent films, nudie-cuties, two children's films, and at least one rural comedy
- Praised by All Movie as a pioneer who went further than anyone else in depicting disgust and discomfort onscreen
