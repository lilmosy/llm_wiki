# Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst, dying in 1368.

## Key Facts
- Died 7 January 1368
- Was the youngest son of Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- His mother was Agnes, daughter of Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal, the second wife of Albert I
- Ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
