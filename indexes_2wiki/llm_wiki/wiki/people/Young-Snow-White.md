# Young Snow White
> The daughter of the original Snow White, who escapes the Wicked Queen's spell and takes refuge with the seven giants in 'A Snow White Christmas'.

## Key Facts
- Daughter of the original Snow White
- Escapes the Wicked Queen's freezing spell
- Takes refuge with the seven giants alongside her dwarf friend
