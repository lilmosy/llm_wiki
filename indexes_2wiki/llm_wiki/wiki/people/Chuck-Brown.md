# Chuck Brown
> American guitarist, bandleader and singer known as 'The Godfather of Go-Go'.

## Key Facts
- Born Charles Louis Brown on August 22, 1936
- Died on May 16, 2012
- Regarded as the fundamental force behind the creation of go-go music
