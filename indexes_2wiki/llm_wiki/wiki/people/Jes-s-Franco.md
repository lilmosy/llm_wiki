# Jesús Franco
> Director of The Blood of Fu Manchu (1968), known for inserting unauthorized stock footage of actress Shirley Eaton into the film.

## Key Facts
- Directed The Blood of Fu Manchu (1968)
- Inserted stock footage of Shirley Eaton from her 1968 film The Girl from Rio into The Blood of Fu Manchu without her knowledge or consent
