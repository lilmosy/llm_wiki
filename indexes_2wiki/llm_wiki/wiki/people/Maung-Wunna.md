# Maung Wunna
> Burmese film director and screenwriter who made his directorial debut with Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella.

## Key Facts
- Directed the 1970 film Wearing Velvet Slippers under a Golden Umbrella
- This was his first feature as director
- Wrote the screenplay for the film, adapting Maung Thara's novel of the same name
