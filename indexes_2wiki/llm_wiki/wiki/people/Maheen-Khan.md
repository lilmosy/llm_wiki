# Maheen Khan
> Pakistani fashion and costume designer known for her fashion labels and work in film and television costume design.

## Key Facts
- Pakistani fashion and costume designer.
- Award-winning fashion designer for labels including 'The Embroidery HouseMaheen' and 'Gulabo'.
- Has participated in numerous national and international fashion events and shows.
- Undertook embroidery work for the film 'Snow White and the Huntsman'.
- Undertook embroidery work for the television series 'The Jewel in the Crown'.
