# Conrad, Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal
> Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal, father of Agnes and maternal grandfather of Waldemar I and Albert II, Princes of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Held the title Margrave of Brandenburg-Stendal
- Was the father of Agnes, who married Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the maternal grandfather of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
