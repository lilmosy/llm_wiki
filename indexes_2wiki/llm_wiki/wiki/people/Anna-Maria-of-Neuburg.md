# Anna Maria of Neuburg
> Countess Palatine of Neuburg who became Duchess of Saxe-Weimar through marriage

## Key Facts
- Born on 18 August 1575 in Neuburg an der Donau
- Died on 11 February 1643 in Dornburg
- Was Countess Palatine of Neuburg
- Became Duchess of Saxe-Weimar by marriage
