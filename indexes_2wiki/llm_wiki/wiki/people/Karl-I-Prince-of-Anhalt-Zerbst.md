# Karl I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Born on 17 November 1534 in Dessau; died 4 May 1561 in Zerbst
- Eldest son of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Mother was Margaret, daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
