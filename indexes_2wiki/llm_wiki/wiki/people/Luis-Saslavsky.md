# Luis Saslavsky
> Argentine film director, screenwriter and producer who was Alberto De Zavalia's partner in the production company SIFAL.

## Key Facts
- Directed and wrote the Argentine crime film Crimen a las tres
- Born April 21, 1903 in Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina
- Died March 20, 1995 in Buenos Aires, aged 91
- Was Jewish
- Directed and wrote over 40 films between 1931 and 1979
- Directed the film Crimen a las tres in 1935
- Wrote the screenplay for Allá en el Norte in 1973
- Retired from the film industry in 1979
- Considered one of the influential directors in the Cinema of Argentina of the classic era
- Was Alberto De Zavalia's partner in running the production company SIFAL
- Completed his second and most famous movie, Crimen a las tres, around the time SIFAL disbanded
- Worked as a film director, screenwriter, and film producer
