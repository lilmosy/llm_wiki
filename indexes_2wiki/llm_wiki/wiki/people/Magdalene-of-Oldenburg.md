# Magdalene of Oldenburg
> Daughter of John VII, Count of Oldenburg, and second wife of Rudolph, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Was the daughter of John VII, Count of Oldenburg
- Was the second wife of Rudolph, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the mother of John VI, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
