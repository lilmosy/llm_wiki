# Countess Palatine Maria Franziska of Sulzbach
> Countess Palatine of Sulzbach who became Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld through marriage

## Key Facts
- Born 15 June 1724 in Schwetzingen; died 15 November 1794
- Fifth child of Joseph Karl, Count Palatine of Sulzbach and Countess Palatine Elizabeth Auguste Sophie of Neuburg
- Became Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld through marriage to Frederick Michael, Count Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld
- Had six siblings, of whom only she and two older sisters, Elisabeth Auguste and Maria Anna, survived to adulthood
- Became Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld by marriage to Frederick Michael, Count Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld
- Of her six siblings, only she and two older sisters, Elisabeth Auguste and Maria Anna, survived to adulthood
