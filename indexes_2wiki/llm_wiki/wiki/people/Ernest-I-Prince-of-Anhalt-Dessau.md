# Ernest I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau
> Prince of Anhalt-Dessau, father of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst

## Key Facts
- Died in Dessau on 12 June 1516
- Was the second son of George I, Prince of Anhalt-Dessau
- First son born by George I's fourth wife, Anna, daughter of Albert VIII, Count of Lindow-Ruppin
- Ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Dessau
- Father of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Married Margarete, daughter of Henry I, Duke of Münsterberg-Oels
