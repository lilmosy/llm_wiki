# Elizabeth Jordan
> Author of the 1919 novel The Girl in the Mirror, which was adapted into the 1920 film The Girl in Number 29.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the 1919 novel "The Girl in the Mirror"
- Her novel was adapted into the 1920 film The Girl in Number 29, directed by John Ford
