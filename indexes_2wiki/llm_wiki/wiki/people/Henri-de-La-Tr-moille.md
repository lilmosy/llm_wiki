# Henri de La Trémoille
> 3rd Duke of Thouars and 2nd Duke of La Trémoille, also Prince of Talmond and Taranto

## Key Facts
- Born 22 December 1598, died 21 January 1674
- Held titles of 3rd Duke of Thouars, 2nd Duke of La Tremoille, Prince of Talmond, and Prince of Taranto
- Son of Claude de La Trémoille and Charlotte Brabantina of Nassau
- Descendant of the medieval general Louis de La Trémoille
