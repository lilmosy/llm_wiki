# Countess Palatine Magdalene Catherine of Zweibrücken
> Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken, first wife of Christian I of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler and mother of Anna Magdalena

## Key Facts
- Lived from 1606 to 1648
- First wife of Christian I, Count Palatine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler
- Mother of Countess Palatine Anna Magdalena of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler
