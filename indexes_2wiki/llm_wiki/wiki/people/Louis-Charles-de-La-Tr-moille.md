# Louis Charles de La Trémoille
> French aristocrat, archivist, bibliophile, and collector who held multiple hereditary titles.

## Key Facts
- Born 26 October 1838, died 4 July 1911
- Held the titles 9th Duke of Thouars, 8th Duke of La Trémoïlle, 11th prince de Tarente, 15th prince de Talmond, and 15th Count of Laval
- Was an archivist, bibliophile, and collector
- Son of Charles Bretagne Marie de La Trémoille and his third wife, Valentine Eugénie Joséphine Walsh de Serrant
