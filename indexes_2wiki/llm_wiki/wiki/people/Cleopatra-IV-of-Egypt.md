# Cleopatra IV of Egypt
> Ptolemaic queen who briefly ruled Egypt jointly with her husband and later became queen consort of Syria.

## Key Facts
- Was Queen of Egypt briefly from 116 to 115 BC
- Ruled jointly with her husband Ptolemy IX Lathyros during that period
- Later became queen consort of Syria as the wife of Antiochus IX Cyzicenus
