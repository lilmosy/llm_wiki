# Waldemar III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst until his death in 1391

## Key Facts
- Died in 1391
- Was a German prince of the House of Ascania
- Ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the youngest son of John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- His mother was Elisabeth, daughter of John I, Count of Henneberg-Schleusingen
