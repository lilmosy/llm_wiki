# Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania who ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst in the 16th century.

## Key Facts
- Born 17 March 1540 in Dessau
- Died 1 March 1570 in Dessau
- Was a German prince of the House of Ascania
- Ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the third and youngest son of John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- His mother was Margaret, daughter of Joachim I Nestor, Elector of Brandenburg
