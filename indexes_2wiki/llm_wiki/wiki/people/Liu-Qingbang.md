# Liu Qingbang
> Chinese writer known for the short novel 'Shen Mu' (Sacred Wood), the basis for the film Blind Shaft.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the short novel 'Shen Mu' (Sacred Wood)
- His novel served as the basis for the 2003 film Blind Shaft
