# Brian Kennedy (gallery director)
> Irish-born art museum director who has led institutions in Ireland, Australia, and the United States

## Key Facts
- Born 5 November 1961
- Irish-born, has worked in Ireland and Australia, now lives and works in the United States
- Currently director of the Peabody Essex Museum
- Director of the Toledo Museum of Art in Ohio from 2010 to 2019
- Director of the Hood Museum of Art from 2005 to 2010
- Director of the National Gallery of Australia (Canberra) from 1997 to 2004
