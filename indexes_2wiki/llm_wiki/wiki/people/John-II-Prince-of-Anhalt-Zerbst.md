# John II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania, father of Waldemar III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst

## Key Facts
- Died on 11 April 1382
- Was a German prince of the House of Ascania and ruler of the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was the youngest son of Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- His mother was Beatrix, second wife of Albert II and daughter of Rudolf I, Elector of Saxony
- Was Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Married Elisabeth, daughter of John I, Count of Henneberg-Schleusingen
- Was the father of Waldemar III, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
