# James Cruze
> Silent film actor and director born Jens Vera Cruz Bosen, active in early Hollywood cinema.

## Key Facts
- Directed the 1930 film Once a Gentleman
- Born Jens Vera Cruz Bosen on March 27, 1884 near Ogden, Utah
- Died August 3, 1942 in Hollywood, California
- Worked as a silent film actor and film director
