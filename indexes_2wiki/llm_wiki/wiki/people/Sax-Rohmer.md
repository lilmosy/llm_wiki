# Sax Rohmer
> Author who created the fictional villain Dr. Fu Manchu, on whom The Blood of Fu Manchu is based.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the 1932 novel The Mask of Fu Manchu
- The novel was adapted into the 1932 film of the same name
- Created the fictional Asian villain Dr. Fu Manchu
- The Blood of Fu Manchu (1968) is based on his character
