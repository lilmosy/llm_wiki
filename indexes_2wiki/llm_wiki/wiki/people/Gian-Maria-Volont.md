# Gian Maria Volonté
> Italian actor who starred as the lead in both 'The Mattei Affair' and 'The Working Class Goes to Heaven'.

## Key Facts
- Leading actor in 'The Mattei Affair' (1972)
- Also starred in 'The Working Class Goes to Heaven', which shared the Grand Prix with 'The Mattei Affair' at the 1972 Cannes Film Festival
