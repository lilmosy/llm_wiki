# Wicked Queen
> The antagonist of 'A Snow White Christmas' who is unexpectedly revived and casts an evil spell freezing the land.

## Key Facts
- Returns to life unexpectedly during Christmas in 'A Snow White Christmas'
- Casts an evil spell that freezes the entire land
- Is eventually confronted by the seven giants who seek to defeat her forever
