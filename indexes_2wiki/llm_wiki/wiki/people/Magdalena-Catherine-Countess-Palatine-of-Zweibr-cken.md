# Magdalena Catherine, Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken
> Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken by birth who became Duchess and Countess Palatine of Birkenfeld through marriage

## Key Facts
- Born 26 April 1607 in Zweibrücken
- Died 20 January 1648 in Strasbourg
- Was a Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken by birth
- Became Duchess and Countess Palatine of Birkenfeld by marriage
