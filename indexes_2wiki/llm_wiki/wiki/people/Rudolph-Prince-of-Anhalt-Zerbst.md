# Rudolph, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
> German prince of the House of Ascania, ruler of Anhalt-Zerbst and father of John VI.

## Key Facts
- Married Magdalene, daughter of John VII, Count of Oldenburg, as his second wife
- Was the father of John VI, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Ruled the principality of Anhalt-Zerbst
