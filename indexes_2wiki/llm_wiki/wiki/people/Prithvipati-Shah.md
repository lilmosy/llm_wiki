# Prithvipati Shah
> King of the Gorkha Kingdom in present-day Nepal who died in 1716

## Key Facts
- Reigned as king of the Gorkha Kingdom until his death in 1716
- Year of birth unknown
- Was the grandfather of Nara Bhupal Shah
- Reigned as king of the Gorkha Kingdom in the Indian subcontinent, present-day Nepal
- Died in 1716; birth year unknown
