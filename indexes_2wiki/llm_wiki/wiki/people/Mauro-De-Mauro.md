# Mauro De Mauro
> Italian investigative journalist who disappeared and was killed by the Sicilian Mafia while researching 'The Mattei Affair'.

## Key Facts
- Was a friend of director Francesco Rosi
- Disappeared while doing research for the film 'The Mattei Affair'
- Was killed by the Sicilian Mafia
- His case, like Enrico Mattei's death, was never solved
- 'The Mattei Affair' includes footage of Rosi searching for him
