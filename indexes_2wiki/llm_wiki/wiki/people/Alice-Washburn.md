# Alice Washburn
> American stage and film actress active in the early 20th century, known for work with Edison, Vitagraph, and Kalem studios.

## Key Facts
- Born in 1860 and died in 1929
- Worked as an actress for the Edison, Vitagraph, and Kalem film studios
- Her final film was Snow White, which was also her only known feature film
- Died of a heart attack in November 1929
