# Pasquale Festa Campanile
> Italian director who made the 1974 comedy film The Gamecock.

## Key Facts
- Born 28 July 1927
- Died 25 February 1986
- Italian screenwriter, film director, and novelist
- Mostly known as a prominent exponent of the commedia all'italiana genre
- Directed the 1974 film The Gamecock
