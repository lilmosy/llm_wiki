# Francesco Rosi
> Italian film director known for the neo-realist and investigative style film 'The Mattei Affair'.

## Key Facts
- Directed 'The Mattei Affair' (1972)
- Developed the concept of cine-inchieste (film investigation)
- Also directed 'Salvatore Giuliano' (1962), whose flashback structure influenced 'The Mattei Affair'
- Remained faithful to neo-realist roots, using on-location shooting and non-professional actors
- Appears in 'The Mattei Affair' searching for his friend, journalist Mauro De Mauro, who disappeared during the film's research
