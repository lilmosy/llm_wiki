# Countess Palatine Dorothea Catherine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler
> Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld-Bischweiler by birth who became Countess of Nassau-Ottweiler through marriage.

## Key Facts
- Born 3 July 1634
- Died 7 December 1715
- Was a Countess Palatine of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld-Bischweiler by birth
- Became Countess of Nassau-Ottweiler by marriage
