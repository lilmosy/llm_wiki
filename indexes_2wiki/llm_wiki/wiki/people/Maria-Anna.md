# Maria Anna
> Elder sister of Maria Franziska of Sulzbach who survived to adulthood

## Key Facts
- Daughter of Joseph Karl, Count Palatine of Sulzbach and Countess Palatine Elizabeth Auguste Sophie of Neuburg
- One of only three of seven siblings to survive to adulthood, alongside Maria Franziska and Elisabeth Auguste
