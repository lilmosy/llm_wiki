# Brothers-Grimm
> German authors and folklorists credited with writing the fairy tale Schneewittchen, the basis for the 1955 film adaptation.

## Key Facts
- Wrote the fairy tale Schneewittchen
- Their story served as the basis for the 1955 German film 'Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs'
