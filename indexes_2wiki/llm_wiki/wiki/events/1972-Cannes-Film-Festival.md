# 1972 Cannes Film Festival
> Edition of the Cannes Film Festival held in 1972, where the Grand Prix was tied between two films.

## Key Facts
- The Working Class Goes to Heaven competed at this festival
- The Grand Prix International du Festival, the festival's highest honour, was tied between The Working Class Goes to Heaven and The Mattei Affair
