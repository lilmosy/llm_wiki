# Snow White (fairy tale)
> A classic fairy tale that served as the basis for two unrelated Filmation sequel productions.

## Key Facts
- Basis for the sequel special 'A Snow White Christmas' (1980)
- Basis for the unrelated sequel film 'Happily Ever After' (1990)
