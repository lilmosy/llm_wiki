# Schneewittchen
> A classic fairy tale by the Brothers Grimm about a princess named Snow White, adapted into multiple films.

## Key Facts
- Written by the Brothers Grimm
- Basis for the 1955 German film 'Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs'
