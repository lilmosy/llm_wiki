# Calloway County High School
> Public high school in Murray, Kentucky formed by consolidating six county high schools.

## Key Facts
- Located in Murray, Kentucky.
- Formed from the consolidation of six high schools across Calloway County: Hazel High School, Lynn Grove High School, Kirksey High School, Almo High School, New Concord High School, and Faxon High School.
