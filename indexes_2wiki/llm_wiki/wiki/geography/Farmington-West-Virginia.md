# Farmington, West Virginia
> A town in West Virginia that serves as the mailing address for North Marion High School, though the school is located closer to Mannington.

## Key Facts
- North Marion High School is located approximately 4 miles from Farmington on US Route 250 north
- Serves as the mailing address for North Marion High School despite the school being closer to Mannington
