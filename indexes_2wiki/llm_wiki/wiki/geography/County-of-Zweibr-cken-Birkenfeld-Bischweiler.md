# County of Zweibrücken-Birkenfeld-Bischweiler
> A branch territory of the Palatinate ruled by the House of Wittelsbach, into which Dorothea Catherine was born.

## Key Facts
- Was the birth domain of Countess Palatine Dorothea Catherine, born 1634
