# Anhalt-Zerbst
> Principality of the Holy Roman Empire ruled by members of the House of Ascania, including Albert I, Albert II, Bernhard VII, John V, Rudolph, and John VI.

## Key Facts
- Was ruled by Waldemar I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst until his death in 1368
- Was ruled by Albert I, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, father of Waldemar I
- Was ruled by John V, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was ruled by Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, who was born in 1540 and died in 1570
- Was ruled by Rudolph, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst
- Was ruled by John VI, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst in the 17th century
- Was ruled by Albert II, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst, who died in 1362
