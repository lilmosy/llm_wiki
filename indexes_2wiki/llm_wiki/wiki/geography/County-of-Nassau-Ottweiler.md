# County of Nassau-Ottweiler
> A county held by the House of Nassau, whose countess title was acquired by Dorothea Catherine through marriage.

## Key Facts
- Dorothea Catherine of Birkenfeld-Bischweiler became Countess of Nassau-Ottweiler through marriage
