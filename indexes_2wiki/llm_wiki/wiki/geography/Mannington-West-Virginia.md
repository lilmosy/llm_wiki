# Mannington, West Virginia
> A city in West Virginia that North Marion High School is closer to, and the real-world basis for the fictional town of Grantville in the novel "1632".

## Key Facts
- North Marion High School is closer to Mannington than to Farmington
- The fictional town of Grantville in the novel "1632" is based on the real town and surroundings of Mannington
