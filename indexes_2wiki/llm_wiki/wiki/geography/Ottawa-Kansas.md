# Ottawa, Kansas
> City in Kansas where the historic Ottawa High School and Junior High School buildings are located.

## Key Facts
- Home to the Ottawa High School and Junior High School, located at 526 and 506 S. Main St.
- The Ottawa High School building was the eighth school built in the city
- A new high school was built in the city in 1966 for upper-class students
