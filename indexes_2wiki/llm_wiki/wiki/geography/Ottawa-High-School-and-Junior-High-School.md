# Ottawa High School and Junior High School
> Historic former high school and junior high school buildings in Ottawa, Kansas, listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

## Key Facts
- Located at 526 and 506 S. Main St. in Ottawa, Kansas
- The high school building was built in 1917
- The junior high school building was built from 1927 to 1928
- An enclosed hallway connecting the two buildings was built along with the junior high school
- The high school was the first school in Ottawa built solely as a high school and the eighth school built in Ottawa
- Designed in the Collegiate Gothic style by George P. Washburn & Son (later Washburn & Stookey)
- In 1966, a new high school was built for the upper three classes of students
- The freshman class moved to the new building in 1976, and the old buildings became a middle school
- The two original buildings were connected by a multi-purpose room in 1976
- The middle school later vacated the building
- Added to the National Register of Historic Places on October 6, 2000
