# Dessau
> City that was the birthplace and deathplace of Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst.

## Key Facts
- Was the birthplace of Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst on 17 March 1540
- Was the deathplace of Bernhard VII, Prince of Anhalt-Zerbst on 1 March 1570
